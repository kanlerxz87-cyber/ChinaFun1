# ChinaFun1 — China & Hong Kong 2027 Travel Atlas

Mobile-first static GitHub Pages research dashboard and verified data ledger for a **7-night Feb–Mar 2027 trip for 2 adults** across **Hong Kong, Shanghai, and Beijing**.

## Executive Summary (Verified Nov 25, 2026)

- **#1 Overall City:** **Hong Kong** — **234 MICHELIN Guide venues (78★)** with compact MTR reach where all 12 audited MICHELIN restaurants and all 8 curated attractions sit &le;30 minutes from every audited hotel.
- **#1 Overall Luxury Base:** **Mandarin Oriental, Hong Kong** (`$5,302` Feb 15–22) — **11/12 audited MICHELIN venues &le;15 min** door-to-door.
- **#1 Overall Best-Value Base:** **iclub Sheung Wan Hotel** (`$727` Feb 15–22) — **8/12 audited MICHELIN venues &le;15 min** at ~1/7th the luxury price.
- **#1 Mainland Value-Luxury Base:** **The Kunlun Jing An (Shanghai)** (`$659` Feb 15–22, free cancellation) — lowest 7-night price across all 15 audited hotels with 5★ Jing'an 3-line subway connectivity.
- **#1 Beijing Base:** **Waldorf Astoria Beijing** (`$2,246` Feb 15–22 / Mar 1–8) for Lines 5 + 8 reach; **Park Plaza Wangfujing** (`$833`, 323 sq ft) for value space.
- **Cheapest 7-Night Window:** **Feb 15–22, 2027** beats Mar 15–22 across 13 of 14 dynamic-rate properties by **7%–40.5%**.
