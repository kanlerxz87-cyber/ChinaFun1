Critical booking & closure rules for 15–22 Feb 2027

Monday 15 Feb 2027 closures

Do not schedule these on arrival Monday: Palace Museum, National Museum of China, Temple of Heaven inner halls (Hall of Prayer / Echo Wall / Circular Mound), Summer Palace inner courtyards, Tian'anmen Rostrum, and most 798 indoor galleries. Instead, Yonghe Temple, Jingshan Park, Tian'anmen Square, Nanluoguxiang, and Temple of Heaven outer park are open Mondays.

Real-name passport reservations

Palace Museum (7 days ahead at 20:00, NO walk-up tickets), National Museum (international passport + email portal up to 5 visitors; grants same-day morning/afternoon Square access), Tian'anmen Square (Jingtong / mini-program), and Yonghe Temple (up to 10 days ahead, 40k cap) require original passports at entry.

Why Wangfujing / Dongcheng wins the ≤1 hour rule: From Wangfujing, 9 of 9 central sights sit within 1 hour by subway (including Summer Palace at 51 min via Xidan → Line 4 and 798 Art Zone at 54 min via Dawanglu → Line 14 on Rome2rio). From Guomao CBD in Chaoyang, Summer Palace is 58 min subway / 1h 07m bus+metro (borderline >1h door-to-door). Only Mutianyu Great Wall (Huairou, ~70 km / ~90 min road) sits outside 1 hour from any city hotel.
