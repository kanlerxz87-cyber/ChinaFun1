Critical booking & closure rules for 15–22 Feb 2027

Monday 15 Feb 2027 closures

Do not schedule these on arrival Monday: Palace Museum, National Museum of China, Temple of Heaven inner halls (Hall of Prayer), Summer Palace inner courtyards, Tian'anmen Rostrum, and most 798 indoor galleries. Instead, Yonghe Temple, Jingshan Park, Tian'anmen Square, Nanluoguxiang, and Temple of Heaven outer park are open Mondays.

Real-name passport reservations

Palace Museum (7 days ahead at 20:00, NO walk-up tickets), National Museum (1–7 days ahead at 17:00), Tian'anmen Square (1–7 days ahead — separate from Forbidden City!), and Yonghe Temple (up to 10 days ahead, 40k cap) all require original passports at entry.

Why Wangfujing / Dongcheng wins the ≤1 hour rule: From Wangfujing, 9 of 9 central sights sit within 1 hour by subway (including Summer Palace at 48 min via Line 1 → 4 and 798 Art Zone at 55 min via Line 1 → 14). From Guomao CBD in Chaoyang, Summer Palace takes 1h 05m. Only Mutianyu Great Wall (Huairou, ~90 min road) is outside 1 hour from any city hotel.
