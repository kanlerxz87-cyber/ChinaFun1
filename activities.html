Key low-season (15–22 Feb 2027) & closure highlights

Central Imperial Axis (Monday closures apply)

  • Palace Museum (Forbidden City): Tue–Sun 08:30–16:30 (last 15:30), ¥40/pp (¥80 for 2) + ¥10 Treasure / ¥10 Clocks; daily cap 40,000, passport booking, closed Mon.
  • National Museum of China: Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00 (last 16:00), Free w/ passport reservation (grants same-day morning/afternoon Tian'anmen Square access), closed Mon.
  • Tian'anmen Square: Daily 05:00–22:00, Free w/ real-name slot booking (Rostrum CNY 15).
  • Jingshan Park: Low season 06:30–20:00 (last 19:30), CNY 2/pp regular (CNY 10/pp exhibition).

Temples, Gardens, Art & Great Wall

  • Temple of Heaven: Low season park 06:30–22:00, inner halls 08:00–17:00 (closed Mon); CNY 10 park / CNY 28 combo per adult.
  • Yonghe Temple: Low season 09:00–16:30 (ticket cutoff 16:00), CNY 25/pp (¥50 for 2); confirm Lantern Festival schedule for 20 Feb.
  • Summer Palace (Haidian): Low season park 06:30–19:00, courtyards 08:30–17:00 (closed Mon), CNY 20/pp; allow >1 hr transit from Wangfujing.
  • Mutianyu Great Wall (Huairou): Winter 08:00–17:30 (sales end 16:30), CNY 40–45 entry + shuttle RMB 15 + cable car RMB 140 RT; full-day road trip.
  • 798·751 Art Zone & Nanluoguxiang: Grounds/streets open all day free; many 798 galleries close Mondays.

Travel-time rule for the activity plan

The brief asks for ≤1 hour subway/bus travel time per activity from the hotel. Because live door-to-door Chinese routing requires Amap (高德) or Baidu Maps and cannot be certified from static web pages, this planner never prints invented minutes. Instead, the itinerary keeps each day inside one district, and two excursions — Mutianyu (~70 km, Huairou) and Blackswan (Capital Airport Rd, Shunyi) — plus Summer Palace (Haidian) are explicitly flagged as likely >1 hour from Wangfujing.

Calendar checkpoints (Feb 2027)

  • 5–13 February 2027 — Spring Festival holiday window (secondary calendar; State Council primary notice not located). Trip begins cleanly on 15 Feb.
  • Sunday 14 February 2027 — reported make-up workday; avoid flying into heavy domestic rail/road traffic early.
  • Monday 15 February 2027 — Palace Museum, National Museum, Temple of Heaven inner altars, and Summer Palace inner halls are all closed Mondays. Start with Nanluoguxiang / Tian'anmen / hotel check-in.
  • Saturday 20 February 2027 — Lantern Festival; Yonghe Temple and temple-fair areas will be busiest.
