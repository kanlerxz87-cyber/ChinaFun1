The two that are fully verified

Palace Museum (Forbidden City)

  • Open Tuesday to Sunday; closed Mondays except national holidays and Chinese New Year's Eve.
  • Hours, 1 Nov – 31 Mar: 8:30–16:30, last entry 15:30.
  • Ticket: ¥40 low season. Treasure Gallery +¥10, Gallery of Clocks +¥10.
  • Booking: no same-day tickets, daily cap 40,000, passport number required for foreign visitors.
  • Why it matters here: 15 February 2027 is a Monday, so day one cannot be the palace.

Source: intl.dpm.org.cn/visit.html ↗

Mutianyu Great Wall

  • Off-season, 16 Nov – 15 Mar: 8:00–17:30.
  • Last: ticket sales end 16:30, gate check-in 8:00–17:00.
  • Why it matters here: the whole February trip sits inside the winter timetable, so a late start loses the day.
  • Not verified: ticket and cable-car prices — the English page read in this pass does not publish them.

Source: en.mutianyugreatwall.com ↗

Travel-time rule for the activity plan

The brief asks for an hour or less of travel per activity, measured from the hotel. That number cannot be certified from a website — it depends on the line you take, the interchange and the time of day — so this planner does not print invented minutes. Instead the itinerary groups each day into a single district, which keeps every hop inside one area, and each restaurant row links to its own official page and address so a route can be checked in Amap (高德) or Baidu Maps before booking.

Distances that are definitely outside an hour from a Wangfujing base: Mutianyu (Huairou district, a half-day trip by road) and Blackswan (Capital Airport Road, Shunyi). Both are treated as planned excursions, never as "pop over after dinner".

Days to avoid in this window

  • Sunday 14 February 2027 — a make-up workday before the trip; expect a heavy domestic travel day if arriving early.
  • 5–13 February 2027 — the Spring Festival holiday. The trip deliberately starts after it ends.
  • Every Monday — the Palace Museum is closed, so a Monday arrival should be a walking/hutong day.
  • Saturday 20 February 2027 — Lantern Festival. Nice to be in the city for, but the temple fairs and Yonghe Temple area will be at their busiest.
