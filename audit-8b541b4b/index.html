Independent review: before you book

Reviewed 25 November 2026. This is a targeted audit, not a line-by-line verification of all four existing plans. The source links below allow manual review. Dates, prices and opening hours can change.

Executive summary

The repository already has four independent planners. The working week, 15–22 February 2027, is seven nights; the preferred 15–18 February window is only three nights. A verified hotel ranking by restaurants reachable within one hour is not yet available. A district count or a simulated transit time is not an actual subway or bus route. Do not treat any plan's price or hotel ranking as booking-ready.

One root-page museum exception is reversed; an older hotel row does not match the room now displayed on its dated search. These and other problems require integration into the original pages by their owners. The evidence-backed examples below do not imply that the remaining entries are verified.

Next decisions and improvement proposals

  1. Destination and budget: compare Beijing and Shanghai after actual room and transit evidence is available; flight origin, total budget and traveller preferences are unknown.
  2. Hotel and timing: collect comparable official, dated seven-night quotes for two with bed, private bathroom, taxes and cancellation terms. A few snapshots cannot establish the cheapest booking time or cheapest week across the entire window.
  3. Transit: verify each actual route for the intended time and date before using the one-hour cutoff; retain the originating route and time.
  4. Coverage: individually verify every activity and restaurant row, hours, exceptions, prices and direct review videos. Search-results links are discovery tools, not evidence that a matching review exists.
  5. Integration: correct conflicting claims on original pages and publish flags in the original review pages after their owners release the files. Do not merge the conflicting studies as if their methods were equivalent.

These are proposals, not claims that the work is complete. Unknowns must remain unverified rather than guessed.

This audit is independent of the existing pages and is not a representation that their full data has been rechecked.
