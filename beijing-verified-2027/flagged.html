Activity flags

PlaceFlag
The Palace Museum (Forbidden City)The 40,000 cap is the operator's own published figure and is unusually low for a site this size in a holiday period - book the moment the window opens. Lantern Festival (20 Feb 2027) falls inside the trip.
National Museum of ChinaReservation rules are strict and quota-limited (1 booking per account per week). Book this and the Forbidden City on different days, or from two accounts.
Temple of Heaven ParkThe official park page publishes its ticket table as images only, so no price could be read as text. Third-party guides quote CNY 10 park entry and CNY 28 combo in winter; treated as unverified and left blank.
Summer Palace (Yiheyuan)Kunming Lake boats are seasonal and were shut from 2 Nov 2026 - a winter visit means walking the whole garden. Inner gardens close on Mondays, which is the day most people would otherwise pair this with the Old Summer Palace.
Mutianyu Great WallThe official ticket page renders its price grid by script, so no price could be read as text and it is left blank. Third-party guides quote CNY 40 admission, CNY 15 shuttle return, CNY 100 cable car one way / CNY 140 return - unverified. Cable car is effectively mandatory in February ice.
Lama Temple (Yonghe Temple)The official site did not respond to fetching. The hours and the CNY 25 ticket shown here are the consistent figure across three independent English guides and MUST be re-checked on the temple's own site before relying on them. Prices left blank on purpose.
Jingshan ParkOfficial site is live (park office phone 010-64038098, 24 h) but publishes no hours or prices in its page text. Nothing could be verified, so every field is blank. It is the single best free-standing view of the Forbidden City roofs and worth 45 minutes if the weather is clear.
Beihai ParkOfficial site is a single-page script app; park office phone 010-64037972 is the only readable fact. Hours and prices blank. Winter means the lake is not boatable and the White Dagoba island ferry is normally suspended.

Source reliability, stated plainly

  • Booking.com is an intermediary, not the hotel. Its own search results carry commission labels and members-only rates. Every price here is linked to the exact dated query so you can re-read it yourself.
  • MICHELIN publishes bands, not prices, and its listings carry no opening days for most Beijing rooms.
  • Two park sites (Temple of Heaven, Jingshan, Beihai) publish prices and hours only as images or script output, so their price cells are blank. Third-party figures are named in the flag but never promoted to verified.
  • Instagram and TikTok are not reliably readable by automated fetching; only what rendered was recorded.

Method. Each value was read from the official page linked in its own row. Anything that could not be read from an official page is blank and carries a flag — it is never estimated and never copied from a blog.
