7-night totals on real dates

Six 5-star hotels, priced on real dates

Every figure below is a 7-night total for 2 adults in 1 room, read off a dated Booking.com page on 2026-11-25. Two windows were priced: the preferred week Mon 15 – Mon 22 Feb 2027 and a control week Mon 1 – Mon 8 Mar 2027. The point of the second window was to test the assumption that going later is cheaper. It is not, uniformly.

Why there is no single “ranked list” yet. Ranking needs your weights for price against location against amenities, and the honest answer is that only one hotel could be priced like-for-like in both weeks. Rather than invent a scoring formula and dress it up as objectivity, the table gives you the verified numbers and the trade-offs in plain words below it.

The one clean comparison

New World Beijing Hotel is the only property priced as the same room type in both windows: Superior Room King, 1 king bed, private bathroom with walk-in shower and deep soaking tub. Public rate $1,177 for 15–22 Feb against $1,257 for 1–8 Mar before tax. February is cheaper by $80 on the identical room. Cheaper figures exist ($901 and $961.23) but both require a signed-in account, so they are noted and excluded rather than used.

What each hotel actually costs you in trade-offs

Hilton Beijing Wangfujing — $1,244

The balanced pick. King bed, 8.7 from 904 reviews, Wangfujing, free cancellation with no prepayment. The March week was $1,101 + $183 tax on the standard Deluxe King, so the two weeks are within $40 of each other once the room types are lined up.

New World Beijing — $1,177

Best bathroom of the set and the largest room (538 sq ft). Chongwenmen, Lines 2 and 5, about 10 minutes on foot, 8 minutes to the Temple of Heaven. 9.1 from 1,542 reviews. Slightly further from Wangfujing.

Waldorf Astoria — $2,246

9.2 from 474 reviews, heated pool and steam room, Wangfujing. Nearly double the Hilton for the same postcode. The result card was labelled “Featured — pays us a higher commission”, so its prominence in search results is not evidence of value.

Sunworld Dynasty — $697

The cheapest 5-star found, 9.1 from 4,217 reviews. But the priced room is twin beds, which fails your queen-or-larger rule, and $697 was a members-only rate against $836 public. A king room in the same hotel was not priced.

The Peninsula — price not captured

9.5 from 596 reviews, the highest score in the set, Wangfujing. Its room price was not in the part of the page that could be read, so it is blank rather than guessed.

Grand Hyatt — excluded

The page loaded in Portuguese with a non-refundable apartment rate ($1,568 + $260 tax). That is not comparable to the refundable room-only totals above, so it is recorded as a note and left out of the table.

Cheapest upcoming time to book — what can honestly be said

Two windows is not a price curve. What the data supports is narrower than a recommendation, and it is worth stating plainly instead of overclaiming:

  • For the one like-for-like room, your preferred February week is cheaper than the early-March control week.
  • 15 Feb 2027 is the Monday after the Spring Festival holiday (Golden Week ran 6–13 Feb 2027, with 14 Feb a make-up working day). Demand collapses right after that, which is consistent with February pricing well.
  • Free-cancellation cut-offs cluster around 7 days before arrival, so a rate can be held now and re-checked later at no cost — that is the actual cost-control lever here, not a forecast.
  • Not verified: whether prices fall further later in March, or whether a 21-day stay is cheaper per night. Neither was priced.

Method. Each value was read from the official page linked in its own row. Anything that could not be read from an official page is blank and carries a flag — it is never estimated and never copied from a blog.
