Three things worth knowing before you read a table

  1. Your dates are good. Spring Festival 2027 runs 6–13 Feb; you arrive on the first quiet Monday after it, when crowds and hotel prices both fall.
  2. Your dates are also cold and short. Every venue runs winter hours, the Summer Palace boats stop altogether, and the Mutianyu cable car is the only sensible way up.
  3. February is not automatically the expensive week. The one hotel priced like-for-like in both windows was cheaper in February ($1,177) than in early March ($1,257 before tax).

What is in these files

This build is self-contained in beijing-verified-2027/: the pages you are reading, plus data/restaurants.json, data/hotels.json and data/activities.json. It does not touch the shared Beijing planner at the repository root, which a different session owns. The verification log lists every gap; suggestions lists what to change before booking.

Method. Each value was read from the official page linked in its own row. Anything that could not be read from an official page is blank and carries a flag — it is never estimated and never copied from a blog.
