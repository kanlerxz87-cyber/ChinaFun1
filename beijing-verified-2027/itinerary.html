8 days, 15–22 Feb 2027

Seven nights, built around other people’s closing days

15–22 Feb 2027 is eight days and seven nights, and it starts and ends on a Monday — the one weekday when most of Beijing’s indoor sights shut. The plan below is arranged around that, around the verified winter hours, and around your two preferred criteria: the star restaurants you came for, and not being on a bus at 7am every day.

Timing luck worth knowing. Spring Festival 2027 runs 6–13 Feb with 14 Feb a make-up working day, so your arrival on 15 Feb lands on the first quiet Monday after the holiday. Prices and crowds both drop at exactly that point.

Mon 15 Feb Arrival — Wangfujing, no museums

  • Land, check in, walk Wangfujing and the Donghuamen night-food street.
  • Today is a Monday: the Palace Museum, the National Museum and the Temple of Heaven halls are all shut, so nothing is lost by arriving today.
  • Dinner near the hotel — Fu Chun Ju (PuXuan) is open 17:30–21:30 and is a 1-star Cantonese room inside a Wangfujing hotel; book ahead.

Tue 16 Feb Forbidden City → Jingshan

  • Palace Museum, 08:30–16:30, last entry 15:30. Ticket pre-booked with passport; no on-the-day sales.
  • Exit north to Jingshan Park for the view back over the roofs — unverified hours, so go before 16:00.
  • Dinner: King’s Joy in the Wudaoying hutongs, or Trb Hutong for the exact-price menu.

Wed 17 Feb Temple of Heaven → National Museum

  • Temple of Heaven early, from 06:30 for the park and 08:00 for the halls. Halls open today; they close Mondays.
  • National Museum of China on a timed slot — book exactly 7 days ahead at 17:00.
  • Dinner: Xin Rong Ji (Xinyuan South Road), 3 stars, 17:30–21:00. The 28-day duck needs pre-ordering.

Thu 18 Feb Mutianyu Great Wall

  • Full day, 70 km north. Off-season hours 08:00–17:30 with ticket sales ending 16:30.
  • Cable car up; February ice on the steps is the real risk, not the distance.
  • Back for a late dinner — keep it close to the hotel; Cai Yi Xuan serves until 22:00.

Fri 19 Feb Lama Temple & the hutongs

  • Lama Temple at opening (winter hours end 16:30), then the Confucius Temple and Wudaoying on foot.
  • Lunch at King’s Joy if dinner was elsewhere on Tuesday.
  • Dinner: Lu Shang Lu (2 stars) or Jingji in the Ritz-Carlton.

Sat 20 Feb Summer Palace — Lantern Festival

  • Summer Palace: park 06:30–19:00, inner gardens 08:30–17:00, combo ticket ¥50.
  • No boats in this season — the lake is a walk, not a cruise.
  • 20 Feb is Lantern Festival: expect evening crowds at temples and parks, and prix-fixe menus at several fine-dining rooms. Book dinner before you fly.

Sun 21 Feb 798 / Olympic Park, then the big one

  • Slow morning in 798 Art District or the Olympic Park.
  • Dinner: Chao Shang Chao, the city’s other 3-star room. No published hours — confirm by phone before relying on it.

Mon 22 Feb Departure

  • Monday again: museums shut. Temple of Heaven park opens at 06:30 if you want a last walk.
  • Check-out; the 7-night hotel totals on this site end here.
