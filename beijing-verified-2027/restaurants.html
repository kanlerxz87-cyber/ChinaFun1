What the hours column really says

Only 8 of 33 restaurants publish opening hours on an official page. For the rest the cell is blank, not “open every day”. Two distinct sources exist and they are kept apart in the data file: the MICHELIN listing (4 rows) and the restaurant’s or hotel’s own page (4 rows).

  • Own site / hotel site (strongest): Cai Yi Xuan, Fu Chun Ju, Giada Garden, Il Ristorante – Niko Romito.
  • MICHELIN listing (no closing day stated): King’s Joy, Trb Hutong, both Xin Rong Ji entries.
  • Nobody publishes a closing day. Not one of the eight states a weekly rest day, so every row carries "None published" rather than a guess.

The clusters the hotels are ranked against

Starred restaurants are heavily concentrated. The hotel ranking on the next page counts stars within a one-hour subway or bus ride, so the two numbers that matter are Dongcheng (9 restaurants, 10 stars) and Chaoyang (20 restaurants, 27 stars). Shunyi has Blackswan only, 2 stars, and is far outside a one-hour ring from the centre.
