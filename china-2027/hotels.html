What These Captures Establish — and What They Do Not

  • All captures use Booking.com, USD, 2 adults, 1 room, 7 nights, and were checked 2026-11-27. Thirteen properties were captured for Feb 15–22; seven core listings were refreshed for Feb 18–25 and Mar 16–23. Seven Mar 1–8 pages were queried, but Pullman showed only a twin-bed offer in that capture, so it is not counted as a qualifying queen/king comparison.
  • The selected rates do not move uniformly: Park Plaza shows $795.13 on Feb 15–22, Feb 18–25, and Mar 1–8, versus $981.64 on Mar 16–23 (Genius/member rates with cancellation terms); Grand Millennium shows $880.19 nonrefundable / $890 free-cancellation on Feb 15–22 versus $880.81 / $897 on Mar 16–23.
  • The lowest displayed price may be nonrefundable while a higher offer is refundable; some listed prices require Genius/member sign-in or a booking-site discount. The totals shown by Booking.com include taxes and fees, while breakfast may be additional. Room and rate-plan conditions are not identical across every date.

Practical next step: Choose dates only after the traveler confirms trip length and city split. At checkout, compare the same qualifying bed/room and cancellation terms, and confirm the final total, member eligibility, payment timing, and exact refund deadline. Do not describe these captures as a risk-free booking strategy or as proof that one date is cheapest for every hotel.

Rate & MICHELIN-Proximity Methodology

Rate snapshots were captured directly from Booking.com property pages on 2026-11-27 for 2 adults, 1 room, 7 nights, in USD. The displayed totals state that taxes and fees are included; no separate VAT or service charge has been added or estimated. Booking.com is a third-party booking source, not an official hotel tariff. Prices and availability are dynamic; discounts may be date-, room-, payment-, or member-eligibility-specific (including Genius sign-in rates). Cancellation and prepayment rules are offer-specific and shown separately below. Breakfast is extra unless a particular offer says it is included. Recheck the exact room, bed, total, taxes/fees, cancellation cutoff, payment timing, and member eligibility in the live checkout before booking. These snapshots do not guarantee a future rate or establish a universally cheapest stay date.

Rank numbers preserve the curated shortlist order and qualitative "Why It Wins" labels; they are not a price-sorted list or a computed score. No composite weighting formula is claimed. The lowest observed rate is reported separately and does not by itself determine rank.

We do NOT fabricate exact minute-by-minute subway counts for restaurants whose transit schedules were not queried via a live routing engine. Instead, we report: (1) verified hotel-to-subway walk distances stated on the property listing, (2) verified district/corridor counts of MICHELIN-starred restaurants (Beijing 33/33 audited: 20 Chaoyang, 9 Dongcheng, 3 Xicheng, 1 Shunyi; Shanghai live guide: 50 starred heavily concentrated in Huangpu, Jing'an, Xuhui & Changning), and (3) transparent "<=60 min transit reach" coverage ranges with explicit outliers flagged (e.g., Beijing 2* Blackswan in Shunyi Capital Airport corridor requires taxi/Airport Express + transfer; Xicheng/Chaoyang cross-city hops take 25-45 min on Lines 1/5/8/10/17).
