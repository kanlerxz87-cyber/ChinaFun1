Beijing ↔ Shanghai Rail & Transit Snapshot

  • Fuxing High-Speed Rail (~1,300 km): 4.5–5.5 hr Beijing South ↔ Shanghai Hongqiao. Official 2nd Class CNY 550–670/adult (CNY 1,100–1,340 for 2 one-way); 1st Class ~CNY 1,035; Business ~CNY 2,318 (12306.cn).
  • Beijing Subway: Distance-based CNY 3 (≤6 km) to CNY 6+ (22–32 km); Airport Express CNY 25. New Line 17 central section opened Dec 2025.
  • Shanghai Metro: CNY 3 (≤6 km) + CNY 1/10 km; 1-Day CNY 18 / 3-Day CNY 45; direct international Visa/MC/Amex/JCB tap-to-ride. Flagged: re-verify post-Aug 2026 fare-hearing adjustments for 2027.
  • Dual-City Synergy: Shanghai Museum East is OPEN Mondays (hosting the Met Museum's Jewelry: The Body Transformed show through May 2027!) — solving classic Monday museum closures.
