Beijing ↔ Shanghai Rail & Transit Snapshot

  • Fuxing High-Speed Rail (~1,300 km): 4.5–5.5 hr Beijing South ↔ Shanghai Hongqiao. Official 2nd Class CNY 550–670/adult (CNY 1,100–1,340 for 2 one-way); 1st Class ~CNY 1,035; Business ~CNY 2,318 (12306.cn).
  • Beijing Subway: Distance-based: CNY 3 (<=6 km), CNY 4 (6-12 km), CNY 5 (12-22 km), CNY 6 (22-32 km), +CNY 1 per 20 km beyond 32 km. Capital Airport Express: flat CNY 25.
  • Shanghai Metro: Distance-based (2005 schedule): CNY 3 (<=6 km), +CNY 1 per 10 km. Passes: 1-Day CNY 18, 3-Day CNY 45, Maglev+1-Day Metro CNY 55 (1-way) / CNY 85 (round-trip). Airport Link Line (Hongqiao<->Pudong ~40 min): CNY 4-26. Tap-to-Ride supports international Visa/Mastercard/Amex/JCB contactless cards at gates. Recheck the fare and contactless-card rules before travel; a final 2027 schedule is not established here.
  • Visitor payments: Official Shanghai guidance describes international-card setup for Alipay / Weixin Pay and overseas-card acceptance at participating merchants; card, issuer, app, account limits, and fees vary. Mobile payment guide · Overseas bank-card guide.
  • Dual-City Synergy: Shanghai Museum East is OPEN Mondays (hosting the Met Museum's Jewelry: The Body Transformed show through May 5, 2027!) — solving classic Monday museum closures.
