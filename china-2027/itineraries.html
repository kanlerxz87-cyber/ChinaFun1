Day 1 · Mon Feb 15Arrival, Hutongs & Sunset Panorama (Zero Monday Closures)
  • Morning: Arrive PEK/PKX -> check into Dongcheng/Chaoyang base (Airport Express CNY 25 + subway CNY 3–5).
  • Afternoon: Stroll Nanluoguxiang & quiet side hutongs (open 24/7, free); late-afternoon climb at Jingshan Park (06:30–20:00, CNY 4 for 2) for golden-hour views over Forbidden City.
  • Evening: Casual Bib Gourmand dinner at No. 69 Fangzhuanchang Zhajiangmian or splurge welcome dinner at 1* Zijin Mansion (17:30–21:30) / 1* Fu Chun Ju (17:30–21:30) — both verified open Mondays.
Day 2 · Tue Feb 16Imperial Central Axis (Book 7 Days Ahead!)
  • Morning: Tian'anmen Square (free w/ advance passport reservation) -> Forbidden City / Palace Museum (Tue–Sun 08:30–16:30, CNY 80–120 for 2; reserve Feb 9 at 20:00 BJT).
  • Afternoon: Exit North Gate -> unwind near Wangfujing / Donghuamen or visit National Museum of China (Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00, free w/ 7-day reservation) if splitting into two museum days.
  • Evening: 1* Trb Hutong (verified Mon–Sun 17:30–22:00; Seasonal menu CNY 3,376 + 15% for 2) OR Peking duck across from Park Plaza at MICHELIN Selected 1949 Duck de Chine.
Day 3 · Wed Feb 17Mutianyu Great Wall Day Trip
  • Morning: Depart 07:30 via direct tourist bus or private car to Mutianyu Great Wall (off-peak 08:00–17:30, base admission CNY 90 for 2; cable car/toboggan extra).
  • Afternoon: Hike towers 14–20 (quietest winter scenery), descend via toboggan/cable car, return to central Beijing by ~16:00.
  • Evening: Restorative Cantonese dinner in Chaoyang at 1* Cai Yi Xuan (Four Seasons, verified 17:30–22:00; Set for Two CNY 1,688 + 10% + 6%) OR 3* Xin Rong Ji (Xinyuan South Rd, verified 17:30–21:00).
Day 4 · Thu Feb 18Temple of Heaven & Chaoyang CBD Fine Dining
  • Morning: Direct Line 5 ride to Temple of Heaven (buy low-season Combo CNY 56 for 2 to enter Hall of Prayer, Tue–Sun 08:00–17:00/18:00).
  • Afternoon: National Museum of China (if not visited Day 2; Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00) OR Qianmen historic street.
  • Evening: Chaoyang CBD MICHELIN highlight: 3* Chao Shang Chao (CP Center), 1* GIADA Garden (China World, Degustation CNY 3,976 for 2), or ¥¥ value 1* The Beijing Kitchen (SKP) / Poetry·Wine (confirm hours by phone).
Day 5 · Fri Feb 19Yonghe Lama Temple & Vegetarian Gastronomy
  • Morning: Arrive 09:00 sharp at Yonghe Temple (daily 09:00–16:30, CNY 50 for 2) on a quieter weekday (avoiding Sat Feb 20 Lantern Festival queues).
  • Afternoon: Walk across street to Wudaoying Hutong cafes; MICHELIN lunch at 2* King's Joy (verified Mon–Sun 11:00–22:00, 2 Wudaoying Hutong) -> afternoon stroll at nearby Confucius Temple.
  • Evening: Relaxed Bib Gourmand dinner at Bao Du Jin Sheng Long (verified 17:00–21:30) or Lao Chuan Ban (Sichuan).
Day 6 · Sat Feb 20 (Lantern Festival!)Summer Palace & Lantern Festival Evening

  • Afternoon: Return mid-afternoon -> explore 798 Art District (free entry; galleries Tue–Sun 10:00–18:00) in Chaoyang.
  • Evening: Lantern Festival night stroll around Qianmen/Shichahai (verify 2027 lantern schedules locally) + dinner at 1* The House of Dynasties (Rosewood, verified 17:30–22:00) or 1* Il Ristorante – Niko Romito (Bvlgari, verified 18:00–22:00).
