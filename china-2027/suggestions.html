SUG-01Booking Strategy (High Savings)Optional Idea — Not Assumed

Hold a Free-Cancellation Black Friday Room Right Now Before Rates Reset

Idea: Reserve base-Beijing Wangfujing ($425/7N, 51% off Black Friday, free cancel to Feb 14) OR Park Plaza Wangfujing ($749/7N Genius, free cancel to 2 PM Feb 15) plus Pullman Jing'an ($707/7N flex) or Radisson Blu Shanghai ($921/7N flex) immediately.

Trade-off: Zero cancellation fee if your dates move; prevents losing $175–$517/week if rooms jump toward mid-March pricing.

SUG-02Route & Date TimingOptional Idea — Not Assumed

Tour Beijing FIRST (Feb 15–22) and Shanghai SECOND (Feb 22–Mar 1)

Idea: Doing Beijing in mid-February avoids early-March NPC/CPPCC ('Two Sessions') security closures around Tian'anmen Square, catches Lantern Festival (Sat Feb 20), and locks Beijing's lowest winter hotel rates.

Trade-off: Beijing in mid-Feb is colder than mid-March (pack thermal layers & windproof coat), though indoor museums/subways are well heated and crowds are far thinner.

SUG-03Beijing Base StrategyOptional Idea — Not Assumed

Consider Splitting or Picking Chaoyang If Fine Dining Outweighs Imperial Sights

Idea: Because Chaoyang holds 20/33 (60.6%) of Beijing's MICHELIN stars vs Dongcheng's 9/33 (27.3%), Grand Millennium ($783–$838/7N) or Kempinski ($845/7N) puts you steps from 3* Chao Shang Chao, 3* Xin Rong Ji, 1* Cai Yi Xuan, and 1* GIADA Garden — while Park Plaza/base-Beijing maximizes Forbidden City/Wangfujing walkability.

Trade-off: Chaoyang adds ~20–30 min subway ride to Forbidden City; Dongcheng adds ~20–30 min subway ride to Chaoyang CBD/Sanlitun dinners.
