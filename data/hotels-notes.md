## B. Cheapest time to book — two separate, verified answers

**Answer 1 — cheapest *stay* window (price-driven):** Mon **2027-03-08 → Mon 2027-03-15** is the cheapest of the three windows tested at the Waldorf ($2,374 vs $2,414 for the preferred week; −1.7%). At the Radisson the price is identical across W1/W2 ($696) because a fixed 30% promotion, not the date, sets it.

**Answer 2 — cheapest time to *book* (promotion-driven, and this is the larger saving):** the Radisson Collection Hyland rate of **$696** is a **30% "Black Friday Deal"** observed live on **2026-11-25**, valid on select stays **Nov 19, 2026 – Dec 31, 2027** — a range that fully covers every date in the requested trip window. Booking that property while the promotion is live captures −$298.35 off $994.49. By contrast the same property's non-promotional rate is $994.49 for the identical 7 nights.

**Decision-relevant trade-off:** at the Waldorf, choosing the preferred week (W1) over the cheapest week (W3) costs **+$40**; choosing free cancellation over non-refundable costs **+$604** (W1: $3,018 vs $2,414). Cancellation flexibility is ~25× more expensive than date optimisation.
