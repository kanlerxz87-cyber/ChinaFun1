# Decision-Making Questions & Trade-Offs (`T5`)

Before locking non-refundable flights, hotels, or Michelin reservations for a 7-night Feb/Mar 2027 trip for 2 adults, answer these 6 core decision questions:

## 1. Culinary Density vs. Value vs. Heritage Monument Scale
- **Hong Kong (#1 City)** wins overwhelmingly on Michelin density (**234 total / 78 starred**) and compact transit (every audited hotel reaches **12/12 audited Michelin restaurants & 8/8 attractions within 30 min**), plus smooth English signage and zero Great Firewall payment friction (Octopus + Visa/Mastercard).
- **Shanghai (#2 City)** delivers the best luxury hotel value in China (**The Kunlun Jing An at $659/week** for 5-star Jing'an access + **153 MICHELIN venues / 50★**).
- **Beijing (#3 City)** offers unmatched UNESCO imperial monuments (Forbidden City, Temple of Heaven, Summer Palace, Great Wall excursion), but ranks #3 for culinary convenience (**107 MICHELIN venues / 33★**, spread across Dongcheng and Chaoyang CBD/Embassy).

## 2. Exact 7-Night Window: Feb 15–22 vs. Mar 1–8 vs. Mar 15–22, 2027
- **Cheapest verified lodging:** **Feb 15–22, 2027** is cheaper across 13 of 14 dynamic-rate hotels — saving **40.5% at HIE Causeway Bay ($766 vs $1,288)**, **35.5% at Upper House SH ($2,352 vs $3,645)**, **25.3% at Atour People's Sq ($685 vs $917)**, **21.5% at MO Wangfujing ($4,582 vs $5,840)**, and **18.4% at Kunlun ($659 vs $808)** / **Waldorf BJ ($2,246 vs $2,754)**.
- **Feb 15–22 Calendar Watch-outs:** Begins **2 days after Mainland Spring Festival holiday ends (Feb 5–13)** and includes **Lantern Festival on Saturday Feb 20, 2027** (some independent Shanghai/Beijing restaurants may have extended LNY breaks on Feb 15–16; expect temple ticket surcharges & Lantern Festival crowds on Feb 20).
- **March Watch-outs:** **Mar 1–8, 2027** overlaps the start of Beijing's **Two Sessions (~Mar 4–14)**, creating Tiananmen/National Museum security disruptions; **Mar 15–22** pushes rates up strongly ahead of HK Easter (**Mar 26–29**).

## 4. Monday & Tuesday Museum Closure Choreography
- All three cities share a Mon–Mon 7-night span (`Feb 15–22`, `Mar 1–8`, or `Mar 15–22`).
- **Closed Mondays:** Forbidden City, National Museum of China, Temple of Heaven interior halls, Summer Palace interior halls, 798 Art District galleries, Shanghai Museum (People's Sq), Yu Garden, West Bund Museum, Long Museum, M+ Museum, plus **Taian Table / Da Vittorio / Bombana**!
- **Closed Tuesdays:** Shanghai Museum East Wing and Hong Kong Palace Museum (**both open Mondays!**).
- **Closed Sundays:** Kau Kee Beef Brisket (HK).
- **Smart Monday Plan:** In Hong Kong, visit **Hong Kong Palace Museum + Tai Kwun + Nan Lian Garden** on Monday; in Shanghai, visit **Shanghai Museum East + Jing'an Temple + Former French Concession** on Monday; in Beijing, visit **Lama Temple + Jingshan Park + Shichahai/Bell & Drum Towers** on Monday.
