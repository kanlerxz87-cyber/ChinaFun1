Suggested order of decisions

  1. Confirm passports and visa status. Nothing else matters if the visa cannot be issued in time.
  2. Fix the week. 15–22 February 2027 is the recommendation: after the Spring Festival, inside the preferred window, and price-flat against March.
  3. Decide the bed rule. Either accept a twin and save up to $1,600 over seven nights, or pay for a king and cut the shortlist to four hotels.
  4. Set the meal ceiling. One exact price exists (Trb Hutong, ¥3,882 for two); the rest are bands, so the ceiling has to come from you, not from the data.
  5. Hold a refundable hotel. Free cancellation was available at 4 of the 12 hotels — take one and keep shopping.
  6. Book The Palace Museum on release day, then the starred dinners in district order so the itinerary stays intact.

Nothing on this site should be booked as-is. The prices are a 25 November 2026 snapshot, the restaurant hours are only complete for 4 of 33 pages, and the direct TikTok/Instagram video links are not yet verified. Read the verification log first.
