Suggested order of decisions

  1. Confirm passports and visa status. Nothing else matters if the visa cannot be issued in time.
  2. Choose Beijing vs Shanghai vs split stay, and fix the week. 15–22 Feb 2027 avoids peak CNY and is price-flat vs March.
  3. Decide the bed rule & district trade-off. Accept a twin ($554–$697) or pick king/queen ($1,075 Happy Dragon / $1,652 Grand Hyatt / $2,246 Waldorf) — or pay Chaoyang premium ($1,753 twin at Four Seasons for 20 in-district stars).
  4. Set the meal ceiling. Fixed two-person benchmarks now exist at Cai Yi Xuan (¥1,958+), Trb Hutong (¥3,882), and Giada Garden (¥3,976 excl. wine).
  5. Hold a refundable hotel. Free cancellation was available at 7 of 11 February hotels (8 of 12 incl. March).
  6. Book Palace Museum, National Museum, Tian'anmen Square, and Yonghe Temple as their windows open (7–10 days out), then lock starred dinners.
