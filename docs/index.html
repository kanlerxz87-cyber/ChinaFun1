Executive summary

Recommendation (v1): base the whole trip in Shanghai, Mon 15 Feb → Mon 22 Feb 2027 (7 nights), and stay at the Radisson Blu Hotel Shanghai New World (88 Nanjing West Road, Huangpu) in a Junior Suite — 1 queen bed, attached private bathroom, US$1,116 total for 7 nights for 2 adults (Booking.com snapshot 25 Nov 2026, before 6.6% VAT).

  • The headline finding — and it is not the one the brief expected. Once you require "MICHELIN-starred, reachable within one hour", every central hotel wins. All four hotels that could be geocoded reach 10 or 11 of the 11 geocodable starred restaurants (2★+) inside the 60-minute band; the only restaurant that ever falls out is Amazing Chinese Cuisine out in Changning, at 55–62 minutes. A one-restaurant swing is smaller than the error in the travel model, so Michelin density does not discriminate between these hotels. Price and room quality do.
  • So the ranking is decided on price: Radisson Blu Junior Suite $1,116 (7 nights) vs Waldorf Astoria 2-queen Deluxe $2,414 vs The Peninsula Deluxe king $3,813. Atour is cheaper at $1,031 but its address could not be verified, so it carries no density score.
  • Dates: the preferred window is also the cheapest of the five 7-night windows priced. The same Radisson room costs $1,295 (22 Feb), $1,485 (1 Mar), $1,560 (8 Mar) and $1,565 (15 Mar) — the preferred week saves $181–$451 on the room alone.
  • Calendar warning: Chinese New Year's Day is Sat 6 Feb 2027; the official Spring Festival break runs 5–13 Feb 2027. The trip begins two days after it ends. Expect residual domestic travel volume and possible restaurant holidays — closure dates are still unconfirmed (see Open Questions).
  • Availability is the real risk, not price: two of the six hotels we priced — The Langham Xintiandi (which has a 2★ restaurant inside the building) and Bvlgari Hotel Shanghai — are simply not bookable on Booking.com for 15–22 Feb 2027. Cheap dates with no inventory is a trap worth knowing before you fall in love with a hotel.
  • What is deliberately blank: menu prices for two (MICHELIN publishes only a ¥–¥¥¥¥ band), opening hours for 9 of the 12 starred restaurants, all attraction ticket prices, and live metro journey times.
