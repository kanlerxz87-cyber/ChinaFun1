Irregularities found and flagged for review

  • Two different Beijing editions circulate right now. The 2026 edition (99 entries, 32 starred) and the 2027 edition (107 entries, 33 starred) are both still indexed across the web. This site uses only 2027 — the edition that will be live during the trip. Any page quoting "32 starred" is a year out of date.
  • Secondary sources already contain errors. A widely syndicated 2026 listicle presents restaurants, addresses and price claims (for example Opera BOMBANA as a three-star, and a combined "Michelin star 2019" list) that do not appear in the current official selection at all. Those claims were not used anywhere on this site.
  • A video link can contradict the current guide. The King's Joy YouTube link describes the restaurant as three-star; it is a 2024 upload and the current official status is Two Stars. The link is kept because it is genuinely useful footage, with a note in the row.
  • Booking's own labels are not always reliable. Swissotel Beijing Hong Kong Macau Center is labelled "Sanlitun, Dongcheng" — Sanlitun is normally described as Chaoyang. Flagged in the hotel table rather than silently corrected.
  • Booking's "5/5" is not a star rating. Several rows show a 5/5 property rating that is a Booking classification, not an official hotel star class.
  • Not everything is loaded yet. Park Hyatt Beijing and Rosewood Beijing are not bookable on Booking for 15–22 Feb 2027 and were excluded rather than priced off an alternate week.
  • One row is deliberately out of period. Purple Horse Garden's $901 quote is for 15–22 March, not February, and is labelled as such wherever it appears.
  • Members-only prices were excluded. New World Beijing showed $901 for signed-in members and $1,177 publicly; the public number is used so the comparison is like-for-like.
  • T12: three official attraction sites were unreachable. tiantanpark.com, summerpalace-china.com and yonghegong.com.cn all returned HTTP 500 on 25 Nov 2026, so those rows stay blank and unverified. Third-party reported hours/prices are recorded in the row notes as unverified, never as facts.
  • T12: 798artzone.com is not an official site. It resolves to a parked/spam placeholder ("fake1@spam.net dummy@trap.org"), so no official link is given for 798. The district is public with no single ticket.
  • T12: King's Joy now has two videos, both saying "3-star". The second video (2026 upload) also describes the restaurant as three-star; the current official status is Two Stars. Both links are kept with the same caveat as before.
  • T12: Xin Rong Ji videos cover only the 3★ branch. The two direct videos are about the Xinyuan South Road three-star location. The Jianguomenwai and Jinrong 1★ branches get labelled search URLs instead, so a video about one branch is never presented as a review of another.
