Irregularities & booking traps flagged

  • Sunworld Wangfujing bed trap: The cheapest $469 Annex Double has a full bed only; we quote the $502 Superior King Room so the queen+ bed rule holds.
  • Old Tower (1★) Mon–Tue closure: Newly starred for 2027, open Wed–Sun 18:00–22:00 only. Never schedule it on Mon 15 or Tue 16 Feb.
  • 2026 vs 2027 edition drift: Older 2024–2026 vlogs call King's Joy 3-star or Shanghai Cuisine 2-star; the live 2027 Guide lists them at 2★ and 1★ respectively.
  • Booking.com tax & Genius variance: Several standard rates exclude 6% VAT until checkout; Genius/Partner discounts depend on signed-in status.
  • Hotel star-class caution: Holiday Inn Express Temple of Heaven is 3–4★ (value IHG), Peking Yard / Orchid are 4★ boutique courtyards, XinQiao / Sunworld are 4★, and NUO / Four Seasons / Waldorf / Park Hyatt / PuXuan / Rosewood are 5★ luxury.
