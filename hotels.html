How to make the ranking defensible

  1. Enumerate current Michelin entries for each city and classify by star, Bib and Selected, without double-counting.
  2. Route each hotel entrance to each restaurant by subway or bus on a specified 2027 date and departure time, including walking and transfers. Record provider and capture time.
  3. Count routes of 60 minutes or less; rank by total, then show starred and non-starred subtotals separately. Do not confuse city totals with local reach.
  4. Collect simultaneous, final seven-night totals for two adults from each official hotel, including taxes and fees, cancellation terms and room type. Compare multiple actual date windows before claiming a cheapest date or booking time.
