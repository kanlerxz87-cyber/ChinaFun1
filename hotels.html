Why the density column is conservative. A district count only includes restaurants whose address was captured and verified from the MICHELIN guide. That is 10 of the 33 starred restaurants: Chaoyang 6, Dongcheng 2, Xicheng 1, Shunyi 1. The true cluster is larger — 23 starred restaurants still need their address captured — so a Dongcheng hotel will likely gain more points than a Chaoyang one once that work is done. The ranking is therefore provisional, and the remaining work is itemised on the suggestions page.

What the hotel set actually shows

Price spread at the same star level

For the preferred week the 7-night totals run $554 → $2,246 — a 4.1× spread across properties that Booking all classes as 5-star. The cheapest four (East Sacred $554, Sunworld Dynasty $697, Purple Horse $901, Swissotel $1,011) are all in walking distance of Wangfujing.

Not everything is bookable yet

Park Hyatt Beijing and Rosewood Beijing returned "unavailable on our site for your selected dates" for 15–22 Feb 2027, and showed only alternative-window prices (Park Hyatt: $2,300.10 for 7 nights starting 13 Feb; Rosewood: $3,119.49 for 7 nights starting 13 Feb). They are excluded from the ranking rather than priced off a different week.

Bed types, not stars, separate the deals

9 of the 12 quoted rates are twin rooms. If a queen/king is mandatory, the honest comparison shrinks to four hotels, and the cheapest of those for February is Happy Dragon Courtyard at $1,075.

Date-window comparison (same hotel, same room)

Grand Hyatt Beijing: $1,652 for 15–22 Feb and $1,652 for 15–22 Mar. Four Seasons Beijing: $1,752.86 for 14–21 Feb vs $1,753 for 15–22 Feb. There is no cheaper week hiding inside the window — which is why the plan keeps the preferred 15–22 Feb dates.

Cheapest upcoming time to book

Two separate questions hide inside that phrase, and the data answers them differently:

  • Cheapest week to stay — flat. Same-hotel comparisons across February and March 2027 returned identical totals, so moving the dates buys nothing. Staying after the 5–13 February Spring Festival is the main calendar win, and that is already true at 15 February.
  • Cheapest moment to click "book" — not answerable from a single day's snapshot. The correct test is to record this same 12-hotel basket weekly and watch the direction of travel; that monitoring task is specced in Suggestions. What the snapshot does show is that free-cancellation rates are available at 4 of the 12 hotels today, and those lock a price without locking the decision.
