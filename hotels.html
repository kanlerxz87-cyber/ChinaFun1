What the hotel set actually shows

Price spread at the same Booking rating

For 15–22 Feb 2027 the 7-night totals span $554 → $2,246 — a 4.1× spread across properties that Booking labels 5/5. The cheapest options (East Sacred $554, Sunworld Dynasty $697, Swissotel $1,011, Prime $1,029) sit in or adjacent to Wangfujing.

Not everything is bookable yet

Park Hyatt Beijing and Rosewood Beijing returned "unavailable on our site for your selected dates" for 15–22 Feb 2027, showing only 13 Feb start quotes ($2,300.10 and $3,119.49). They stay excluded from the ranking rather than priced off a different week.

Bed types & bathroom verification

8 of 11 February-bookable quotes are twin/full rooms or unassigned bed types; only 3 guarantee a king/queen in Feb (cheapest: Happy Dragon Courtyard at $1,075). Private bathrooms were verified individually on 11 of 12 Booking/official pages (e.g., Four Seasons Deluxe Room marble bath verified on fourseasons.com); Prime Hotel did not list "Private bathroom" in the fetched Booking amenity excerpt and is flagged to confirm before booking.

Date-window comparison (same hotel, same room)

Grand Hyatt Beijing: $1,652 for 15–22 Feb and $1,652 for 15–22 Mar. Four Seasons Beijing: $1,752.86 for 14–21 Feb vs $1,753 for 15–22 Feb. There is no cheaper week hiding inside the window — so keeping the preferred 15–22 Feb week sacrifices nothing on price.
