What the verified data says

1 · True 33/33 MICHELIN star geography

With all 33 starred addresses verified from guide.michelin.com, Chaoyang holds 20 (including both 3★ Chao Shang Chao and Xin Rong Ji Xinyuan South Rd plus 3×2★), Dongcheng holds 9 (King's Joy 2★, Fu Chun Ju, Trb Hutong, Zijin Mansion, Zhiguan Courtyard, Lei Garden, The Georg, Mansion Cuisine by Jingyan, DaDong Nanxincang), Xicheng holds 3 (Financial Street cluster), and Shunyi holds 1 (Blackswan 2★ by the airport).

2 · Wangfujing (Dongcheng) beats Chaoyang on the ≤1 hour rule

Rome2rio Beijing Subway schedules show Wangfujing ↔ Guomao CBD★ is just 8 minutes on Line 1 — putting 32 of 33 starred restaurants AND all 9 central city sights (including Summer Palace at 51 min and 798 Art Zone at 54 min) within 1 hour of Dongcheng. From Guomao CBD, Summer Palace is 58 min subway / 1h 07m bus+metro (borderline >1h door-to-door).

3 · 15–22 Feb 2027 beats or ties March at 9 of 11 hotels

Multi-week Booking.com checks (15–22 Feb vs 1–8 Mar & 15–22 Mar 2027) show Feb 15–22 is cheaper or flat at Sunworld Wangfujing ($502 king vs $581/$590), HI Express ($536 Genius / $596 list live vs $505 Mar 15–22), XinQiao ($568 queen vs $511 twin), NUO ($943 flat), Peking Yard ($1,105/$1,163 vs $1,664), Four Seasons ($1,751 vs $1,914), Waldorf ($2,243), Park Hyatt ($2,297 vs $2,589), and Rosewood ($3,115 flat). Only PuXuan ($2,803 Genius Mar) and Orchid Courtyard ($1,025 full bed) dip in early March.

4 · Calendar & Monday closures shape the week

Spring Festival 2027 runs 5–13 Feb (make-up workday Sun 14 Feb per secondary calendars), so Mon 15 Feb lands on the quiet side of the holiday. Remember: Palace Museum, Temple of Heaven inner halls, National Museum, Tian'anmen Rostrum, and 798 galleries close Mondays, and new 1★ Old Tower is closed Mon–Tue (Wed–Sun 18:00–22:00 per Jun 2026 Malay Mail).
