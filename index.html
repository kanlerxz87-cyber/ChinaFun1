What the verified data says

1 · True 33/33 MICHELIN star geography

With all 33 starred addresses verified from guide.michelin.com, Chaoyang holds 20 (including both 3★ Chao Shang Chao and Xin Rong Ji Xinyuan South Rd plus 3×2★), Dongcheng holds 9 (King's Joy 2★, Fu Chun Ju, Trb Hutong, Zijin Mansion, Zhiguan Courtyard, Lei Garden, The Georg, Mansion Cuisine by Jingyan, DaDong Nanxincang), Xicheng holds 3 (Financial Street cluster), and Shunyi holds 1 (Blackswan 2★ by the airport).

2 · Wangfujing (Dongcheng) beats Chaoyang on the ≤1 hour rule

Verified Beijing Subway times show Wangfujing ↔ Guomao CBD★ is just 8 minutes on Line 1 — putting 32 of 33 starred restaurants AND all 9 central city sights (including Summer Palace at 48 min and 798 Art Zone at 55 min) within 1 hour of Dongcheng. From Guomao CBD, Summer Palace takes 1h 05m.

3 · 15–22 Feb 2027 beats March at 8 of 11 hotels

Multi-week Booking.com checks (15–22 Feb vs 1–8 Mar & 15–22 Mar 2027) show Feb 15–22 is cheaper or flat at XinQiao ($485 vs $511 twin), HI Express ($499 vs $536/$505), Sunworld Wangfujing ($502 vs $581/$590), Peking Yard ($1,105 vs $1,664), Four Seasons ($1,751 vs $1,914), and Waldorf ($2,243). Only The Orchid ($1,025 Mar), Park Hyatt ($2,589 Mar), and PuXuan ($2,803 Genius Mar) dip in early March.

4 · Calendar & Monday closures shape the week

Spring Festival 2027 runs 5–13 Feb (make-up workday Sun 14 Feb), so Mon 15 Feb lands on the quiet side of the holiday. Remember: Palace Museum, Temple of Heaven inner halls, National Museum, Tian'anmen Rostrum, and 798 galleries close Mondays, and new 1★ Old Tower is closed Mon–Tue (open Wed–Sun 18:00–22:00).

Recommended base & value picks (queen/king + private bath confirmed)

Best Value King/Queen (Dongcheng): Sunworld Hotel Wangfujing ($502, Superior King, free cancellation) or Holiday Inn Express Temple of Heaven ($499 Genius, 1 queen, breakfast included & free cancellation; XinQiao is $485 non-refundable Partner Offer).

Best Courtyard Boutique (Dongcheng): Peking Yard Boutique Hotel ($1,105 non-ref / $1,163 free cancel, 1 queen, breakfast included, 9.3 rating) or The Orchid Hotel ($1,194 free cancel, breakfast included).

Best 5★ Luxury: Waldorf Astoria Beijing ($2,243 non-ref / $2,582 ref, Wangfujing, indoor pool + 1★ Zijin Mansion on site) for ≤1h access to everything, or Four Seasons Beijing ($1,751 non-ref / $1,945 ref, Chaoyang Liangmaqiao, indoor pool + 1★ Cai Yi Xuan on site) as the lowest-priced ultra-luxury 5-star.
