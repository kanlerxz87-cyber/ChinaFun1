Key Findings

  • Best Hotel Cluster: Hotels in Huangpu (The Bund / People's Square / Yu Garden) and Jing'an sit within 15–45 minutes by Shanghai Metro of Shanghai's highest concentration of Michelin-starred restaurants (including Canton 8, T'ang Court, Imperial Treasure, Bao Li Xuan, and 8½ Otto e Mezzo Bombana).
  • 7-Night Hotel Pricing: Verified sample nightly rates in Huangpu/Jing'an range from ~$99–$144/night (~$693–$1,008 for 7 nights) for upper-midscale/boutique options (e.g., Radisson Collection Hyland, Courtyard by Marriott Shanghai Central) up to ~$321–$525/night (~$2,247–$3,675 for 7 nights) for iconic Bund luxury (Fairmont Peace Hotel, The Peninsula Shanghai). Exact Feb 15–22, 2027 live checkout totals require direct date-specific booking queries.
  • Michelin Dining Landscape (2026 Guide): Shanghai's 2026 Michelin Guide features 1 Three-Star (Taian Table), 12 Two-Star, and 38 One-Star restaurants, plus Bib Gourmand and Michelin-listed venues. Note that Ultraviolet by Paul Pairet closed on March 29, 2025 and was removed from the 2026 guide.
  • Couple Dining Cost: Verified 2-adult totals range from ~404 CNY (~$56) at 2-Star Canton 8 (Huangpu) up to ~4,000–4,800 CNY (~$555–$665) at 3-Star Taian Table.
  • Verification Policy: Every row in this dashboard links to an official or primary source. Any unconfirmed field (e.g., exact 2027 holiday exceptions or live 2027 room inventory) is left blank and listed on Flagged for Review.
