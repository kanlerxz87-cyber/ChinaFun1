What the data says

1 · Chaoyang holds 61% of the stars; Dongcheng holds the sights

With all 33 starred addresses verified, 20 are in Chaoyang (both three-stars, 3 of 5 two-stars, and 15 one-stars), 9 are in Dongcheng (King's Joy 2★ plus 8 one-stars across Wangfujing & the hutongs), 3 are in Xicheng (Chao Shang Chao Xicheng, Furong, Xin Rong Ji Jinrong St), and 1 is in Shunyi (Blackswan 2★ on Capital Airport Road — a planned excursion, not a central walk-up).

2 · Price is flat across the whole window

Grand Hyatt Beijing quoted $1,652 for 7 nights on 15–22 Feb and the identical $1,652 on 15–22 Mar 2027. Four Seasons quoted $1,752.86 for 14–21 Feb and $1,753 for 15–22 Feb — a $0.14 gap. Note that the brief's stated preference (15–18 Feb) is 3 nights, which does not fit a 1–2 week trip, so all plans price a 7-night stay starting 15 Feb.

3 · The bed requirement is the real cost driver

7 of the 11 February-bookable hotel rates are twin rooms and 1 (Regent, $1,102) allocates bed type on arrival. Only three February-bookable properties guaranteed a king/queen bed at the quoted rate: Happy Dragon Courtyard ($1,075), Grand Hyatt ($1,652), and Waldorf Astoria ($2,246) — plus Purple Horse Garden ($901 in March only).

4 · The calendar shapes days 1–6

Spring Festival falls on 6 February 2027; secondary holiday calendars list the official break as 5–13 February with a make-up workday on Sunday 14 February (State Council primary notice not located). Starting Monday 15 February puts the trip past peak travel, but Mondays close the Palace Museum, National Museum, Temple of Heaven inner halls, and Summer Palace inner halls, and winter timetables apply at Great Wall & imperial parks.

Recommended shape of the trip

Base decision (Chaoyang vs Dongcheng): With 33/33 restaurant addresses verified, Chaoyang leads 20 stars to Dongcheng's 9 — lifting Four Seasons Beijing (#1 at 62.0, $1,753 twin, housing 1★ Cai Yi Xuan on-site). However, Wangfujing / Dongcheng holds 11 of the 12 priced 5★ hotels, costs far less ($554–$1,075), puts 9 starred restaurants in-district, and is walkable or 1–2 metro stops to the Palace Museum, Tian'anmen Square, National Museum, and Jingshan Park.

Shortlist as scored: the hotel table puts Four Seasons Beijing first (62.0 — Chaoyang, twin at $1,753), then Happy Dragon Courtyard (35.6 — Dongcheng, $1,075, real queen bed, free cancellation, 9.8 guest rating), then Sunworld Dynasty (33.2 — Wangfujing, $697 twin, free cancellation), then Waldorf Astoria (32.4 — $2,246 king). Because ≤1-hour subway/bus travel times are not yet verified via Amap routing, audit the trade-off before booking.
