What the data says

1 · The stars are already clustered

Of the 10 starred restaurants whose full address is verified, six are in Chaoyang — including both three-star kitchens (Chao Shang Chao (Chaoyang) and Xin Rong Ji (Xinyuan South Road)) and three of the five two-star kitchens (Jingji, Lamdre, Lu Shang Lu). Dongcheng holds King's Joy (2★) and Trb Hutong (1★) inside the hutong belt. Xicheng has one (Xin Rong Ji, Jinrong Street). The only outlier is Blackswan (2★) on Capital Airport Road in Shunyi, which is a day-trip decision, not a walk-up.

2 · Price is flat across the whole window

Grand Hyatt Beijing quoted $1,652 for 7 nights on 15–22 Feb and the identical $1,652 on 15–22 Mar 2027. Four Seasons Quoted $1,752.86 for 14–21 Feb and $1,753 for 15–22 Feb — a $0.14 gap. Shifting the week inside 15 Feb – 23 Mar is therefore not where the savings are; the levers are room type, cancellation terms and how far ahead you book.

3 · The bed requirement is the real cost driver

Every cheapest confirmed rate for the preferred week is a twin. Only four hotels quoted a king or queen bed: Waldorf Astoria ($2,246), Grand Hyatt ($1,652), Happy Dragon Courtyard ($1,075) and Purple Horse Garden ($901 — but only for a March window). Deciding whether a twin is acceptable changes the shortlist more than any other choice.

4 · The calendar matters more than the price

Chinese New Year falls on Saturday 6 February 2027; the official holiday runs 5–13 February, with a make-up workday on Sunday 14 February. Starting on 15 February puts the trip on the quiet side of the holiday. Two hard consequences: 15 February is a Monday, when the Palace Museum is closed, and Mutianyu is still on winter hours (8:00–17:30) until 15 March.

Recommended shape of the trip

Base: Wangfujing / Dongcheng, 7 nights, 15–22 February 2027. It is walkable to the Forbidden City, it is one of the two central districts that actually holds verified stars (Dongcheng, 2), and it holds 9 of the 12 bookable 5-star options we priced — which is what makes a 4× price spread available at the same address quality.

Shortlist as scored: the hotel table puts Four Seasons Beijing first (20.0 — six verified stars in its own district, but the $1,753 rate is a twin room), then Happy Dragon Courtyard (14.6 — $1,075, a real queen bed, 9.8 guest score), then Sunworld Dynasty (12.2 — $697). If you would rather rank on value alone, Sunworld Dynasty wins outright; if the bed rule is non-negotiable, the shortlist collapses to Happy Dragon, Waldorf Astoria ($2,246) and Grand Hyatt ($1,652).
