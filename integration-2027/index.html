What can be decided today

1 · Choose a city or a two-city route

Beijing's 2027 live MICHELIN list shows 33 starred restaurants; Shanghai's live 2026 list shows 50. The official Shanghai regional release gives a different total selection count, retained as a flag. Beijing starred list ↗ · Shanghai starred list ↗

2 · The preferred window does not fit a week-long booking

15–18 Feb 2027 is three nights, while the four single-city price rows cover 15–22 Feb (seven nights). Choose exact travel dates before using any total.

3 · Match room terms before ranking price

The four 25 Nov snapshots mix different room categories, member rates, bed options and tax treatment. Later Beijing and dual-city pages have their own dated captures; the cheapest displayed number is not automatically a queen/king + private-bath match.

4 · A sampled stay date is not a booking-time forecast

The historical comparisons sample stay dates, not a complete booking-lead-time curve. A displayed promotion is evidence of that dated offer only, not proof of the cheapest future day to book.
