Executive summary

Four plans, one decision layer

This repository contains four independent, source-linked trip plans: one Beijing week and three Shanghai studies. They were produced separately, disagree with each other, and are deliberately not merged. This page tells you what each one is for, what is still unverified, and which decision must come first.

What can be decided today

1 · The city comes first

Beijing has 33 starred kitchens in the current 2027 guide. Shanghai's three plans all find that MICHELIN density does not separate central hotels; price and room quality decide. Nothing else in these tables is comparable until the city is chosen.

2 · The preferred window does not fit

15–18 Feb 2027 is three nights. All plans price 15–22 Feb, a seven-night stay. Confirm the length before treating any total as a booking price.

3 · The cheapest room usually fails the brief

Beijing's $554 and Shanghai's $652 rooms are twins. The cheapest king/queen options found are $696 (Shanghai Hyland, selectable king, bathroom unconfirmed) and $1,075 (Beijing Happy Dragon, queen).

4 · Dates are not the main price lever

Every sampled later Shanghai window costs more than 15–22 Feb; one Beijing hotel quoted the same total in February and March. The Radisson's $696 came from a promotion, not from shifting dates.
