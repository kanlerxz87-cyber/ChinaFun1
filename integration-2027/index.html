Executive summary

Five research dashboards, one decision layer

This repository contains four independent single-city studies (one Beijing and three Shanghai) plus a later Beijing–Shanghai dual-city dashboard. They were produced separately and are deliberately not merged; this hub now links all five and keeps their dated findings distinct. Choose a city and exact dates before comparing hotels.

What can be decided today

1 · Choose the city first

The later dual-city study verifies 33 starred Beijing restaurants (2027 edition) and reports 50 starred Shanghai restaurants (2026 live guide). It compares separate city alternatives, not one combined itinerary. Hotel access and the restaurant shortlist depend on this choice.

2 · The preferred window does not fit

15–18 Feb 2027 is three nights, shorter than the requested 1–2 weeks. The current comparable candidate window is 15–22 Feb (7 nights); no 3-night rates are shown here.

3 · Compare compliant rooms only

The 26 Nov dual-city snapshot lists 15–22 Feb examples: Beijing $425 for a queen room with private bath, and Shanghai $621 for a king room with private bath (non-refundable; $707 free cancellation). Both exclude VAT; these city alternatives are not a combined price, and earlier study snapshots differ.

4 · Rates are snapshots, not live quotes

Hotel totals were captured on 25–26 Nov 2026. Room type, promotions, cancellation and tax/service terms differ by listing; use the dated Booking.com source and re-check the exact rate before booking.
