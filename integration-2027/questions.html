Q01 · Beijing or Shanghai? BLOCKING

What we know: The repository now includes four single-city studies plus one dual-city Beijing + Shanghai dashboard. Beijing has 33 starred venues in the 2027 edition; the dual-city study reports 50 Shanghai starred venues in the live 2026 regional guide.

What we do not know: The brief does not choose Beijing, Shanghai, or a dual-city itinerary; the hotel and restaurant totals are not like-for-like.

Action to close it: Choose one city or explicitly choose two; then compare only that city’s room and restaurant shortlist.

Q02 · Which exact dates? BLOCKING

What we know: 15–18 Feb 2027 is a three-night window, shorter than the requested 1–2 weeks. Current candidate dashboards price 15–22 Feb as a seven-night alternative.

What we do not know: Whether the travellers accept a seven-night stay or select different exact dates.

Action to close it: Choose a 7–14-night date range within 15 Feb–23 Mar before using any total.

Q03 · Is one queen-or-larger bed mandatory at the quoted rate? BLOCKING

What we know: A 26 Nov dual-city snapshot lists compliant examples for 15–22 Feb: Beijing $425 queen with private bath; Shanghai $621 king with private bath (non-refundable; free-cancel option $707). Earlier studies quote different rooms and terms.

What we do not know: Which city, cancellation policy, member-rate eligibility, and all-in tax amount apply.

Action to close it: Select the city and exact room/refund terms, then re-quote before booking.

Q05 · Are passports and Chinese visas valid for both travellers? BLOCKING

What we know: State Department page: visa generally required, passport valid 6+ months with blank pages; one study notes registration within 24 hours and at each new address.

What we do not know: Travellers' nationality, visa status and passport dates.

Action to close it: Check both passports and visa eligibility with the official consular source.

Q06 · What is the all-in trip budget ceiling? BLOCKING

What we know: A later dual-city study lists 7-night room snapshots from $425 in Beijing and $621 in Shanghai, excluding VAT; earlier study totals differ by room type, capture date, and tax treatment. Airfare, visa, city transit and most dinners are not priced.

What we do not know: Affordable total for the whole trip and whether member/non-refundable prices count.

Action to close it: Set an all-in ceiling and compare a same-city shortlist on identical dates, room, cancellation and tax terms.

Q10 · What are the official 2027 mainland holiday dates and make-up workdays? open

What we know: The official State Council English page lists the Spring Festival holiday as 5–13 Feb 2027.

What we do not know: That page does not list the proposed 31 Jan and 14 Feb make-up workdays; those dates are not verified by the source cited here. Venue-specific hours and exceptions also remain open.

Action to close it: Locate an official notice or authoritative translation that explicitly lists make-up workdays, then check each operator’s schedule for venue-specific exceptions.
