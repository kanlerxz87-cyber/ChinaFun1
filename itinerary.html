Seven nights that respect the closing days

Two constraints shape the whole week: the Palace Museum is shut on Mondays, and Mutianyu runs on a winter timetable until 15 March. Everything else is arranged so each day stays inside one district — central, then Chaoyang, then central again.

  • Arrival and check-in at the Wangfujing / Dongcheng base.
  • Why not the palace today: the Palace Museum is closed on Mondays — verified on the official site.
  • Evening: a walk through the Wangfujing and Nanluoguxiang lanes. unverified hours — public streets, no ticket.

Swap logic, so the plan survives a sold-out reservation

If a Chaoyang dinner is unavailable

The district has six verified stars: two three-star, three two-star and one one-star. Any of them can take the slot without changing the day's geography.

If the palace is sold out

Tickets are capped at 40,000 a day and carry no same-day sales, so book the moment your date opens. If it fails, the Monday-closed rule means Tuesday is the only realistic fallback in a 7-night stay.

If the weather kills Mutianyu

Move the Great Wall to the sunniest forecast day and push the temple belt into its slot — those two days are independent of each other.

If you add Tianjin

The 2027 guide's new city has 27 entries including one star (China Banquet) and is a high-speed-rail hop from Beijing. It works as a day trip or as a one-night extension, and it costs one Chaoyang dinner.

What this itinerary does not claim. It does not claim a certified travel time for any leg, it does not claim ticket prices for the unverified activities, and it does not claim that a starred restaurant will have a table. Those three gaps are exactly the open items on the questions page.
