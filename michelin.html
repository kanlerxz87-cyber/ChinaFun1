What changed in the 2027 edition

  • New stars: Chef 1996 (promoted from Selected to One Star, Sichuan) and Old Tower (One Star on debut, chef Talib Hudda).
  • Retained three stars: Chao Shang Chao (Chaoyang) and Xin Rong Ji (Xinyuan South Road).
  • Retained two stars: Blackswan, Jingji, King's Joy, Lamdre, Lu Shang Lu.
  • Moved down a tier: Shanghai Cuisine held Two Stars in 2026 and holds One Star in the 2027 selection.
  • Tianjin debuted alongside Beijing: 27 entries including 1 One-Star (China Banquet), noted as an optional rail excursion (timetable not verified here).
