What changed in the 2027 edition

  • New stars: Chef 1996 (promoted from Selected to One Star, Sichuan) and Old Tower (One Star on debut, contemporary pescatarian tasting menus from chef Talib Hudda).
  • Retained three stars: Chao Shang Chao (Chaoyang) and Xin Rong Ji (Xinyuan South Road).
  • Retained two stars: Blackswan, Jingji, King's Joy, Lamdre, Lu Shang Lu.
  • Moved down a tier: Shanghai Cuisine held Two Stars in the 2026 edition (confirmed in the official 2026 release) and appears as a One Star in the current 2027 listing. Chao Shang Chao (Xicheng) was newly awarded One Star in 2026 and keeps that One Star in 2027.
  • New to the guide entirely: Tianjin, released alongside Beijing with 27 restaurants including one star (China Banquet). It is connected to Beijing by high-speed rail, which makes it a plausible day trip — the timetable was not verified in this pass.
