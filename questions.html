Q1  Which 7-night window should be booked?

Impact: BLOCKING — no hotel can be reserved until this is answered

What is already verified: Three windows were priced with live quotes for the identical room. Preferred 15–22 Feb = $2,414 at the Waldorf; 22 Feb–1 Mar = $2,378; 8–15 Mar = $2,374. The Lantern Festival falls on Sat 20 Feb 2027, inside the first window only.

What is not known: Whether the $40 saving is worth losing the Lantern Festival, and whether travel dates are fixed by work.

How to discover it: Compare the three quotes on Costs. Decide against the calendar on Dates & Entry. No further research is needed — this is a preference decision, not an information gap.

Q2  What is the official mainland public-holiday calendar for 2027?

Impact: BLOCKING for non-refundable bookings

What is already verified: Secondary sources report the Spring Festival break as 5–13 Feb 2027 with make-up working days on Sun 31 Jan and Sun 14 Feb, citing a State Council notice of 5 Nov 2026. Another secondary source says the mainland 2027 calendar had not been published as of 5 Nov 2026 and that private calendars contradicted each other.

What is not known: The primary State Council notice was not located in this session. Venue rules that turn on “except national holidays” (Yu Garden, both museums) cannot be resolved with certainty for the 14 Feb make-up day.

How to discover it: Search the State Council portal (www.gov.cn) for the 2027 holiday notice, and the official National Immigration Administration or Chinese embassy site for the same. Then re-read each venue's “except public holidays” clause against the confirmed calendar.

Q3  Does the Radisson Collection Hyland's Collection Superior Room have a private bathroom?

Impact: BLOCKING for the $696 option

What is already verified: The rate page confirms: 269 ft², “Select your bed (if available) — 1 king bed or 2 twin beds”, max 2 people, air conditioning, soundproofing, free cancellation until 14 Feb 2027, no prepayment.

What is not known: Bathroom type is not stated in the fetched rate text. The requirement is a private bathroom, so this must not be assumed.

How to discover it: Open the same dated quote and expand the room's “What's in the room” list, or email or call the property directly and ask for written confirmation of an en-suite private bathroom for the king configuration. Record the written answer in data/hotels-notes.md.

Q4  What do hotels in the Zhenning Road corridor cost?

Impact: HIGH VALUE — the corridor holds the only 3★

What is already verified: Taian Table (3★) is at No.161, Lane 465, Zhenning Road, Changning, and Fu 1088 (1★) at 375 Zhenning Road, Jing'an — the same street. The official 2026 launch release adds 菁禧荟 (2★, Changning) and 三月醉淮扬 (1★, Jing'an).

What is not known: No hotel in Jing'an or Changning was priced in this session, so the corridor cluster cannot be compared against Huangpu on cost.

How to discover it: Run the same dated Booking query pattern used for the four priced hotels — checkin=2027-02-15&checkout=2027-02-22&group_adults=2&no_rooms=1&sb_price_type=total&type=total&selected_currency=USD — against Jing'an and Changning properties, and record each quote with its fetch timestamp.

Q5  What are the current Shanghai Museum East hours?

Impact: MEDIUM — affects the Tuesday plan

What is already verified: The official notice dated 2024-09-10 gives 10:00–18:00, last entry 17:00, closed every Tuesday except public holidays, walk-in with valid ID at the B1 East Gate.

What is not known: The current visit-east page was not fetched. The People's Square page has since changed materially (last entry moved from 16:00 to 15:00 and advance booking was reintroduced), so the East page may also have moved.

How to discover it: Fetch https://www.shanghaimuseum.net/mu/frontend/pg/m/en/service/visit-east and compare against the 2024 notice; if they differ, replace the value and log the change in COORDINATION.md.

Q6  What does the Shanghai Tower observatory cost, and when is it open?

Impact: MEDIUM — a headline activity with no verified price

What is already verified: The operator's official page publishes the route (B1 exhibition hall, 118F observatory at 546 m, SKY632 on 125F/126F), the inquiry hotline (8621)80370150 with inquiry hours Mon–Sun 9:00–18:00, the instruction to carry ID or passport, and the official ticket channels Ctrip.com, LY.com, Lvmama.com, Meituan.com and Dianping.com.

What is not known: Neither the ticket price nor the observatory's opening hours appear on the official page. Widely quoted third-party figures exist but are not official, so they are excluded.

How to discover it: Check the five official ticket channels named by the operator, or call the hotline. Record the price, the hours and which channel quoted them, then update data/activities.csv.

Q7  What are the door-to-door transit times from each hotel to each venue?

Impact: BLOCKING for the ≤1-hour requirement as literally worded

What is already verified: Shared district is verified for every Huangpu hotel and the Huangpu venues. The Shanghai Tower operator officially publishes metro routes and journey times, including 40 minutes from Shanghai Railway Station South Square via Line 1 to People's Square then Line 2 to Dongchang Road.

What is not known: No official journey planner or routing source was reachable, so no specific hotel-to-venue minute figure is asserted anywhere.

How to discover it: Use the Shanghai Metro official journey planner or Amap/Baidu Maps transit directions for each hotel-to-venue pair, capture the published door-to-door minutes and the lines used, and add them as a new verified column. Until then the site ranks on shared district plus the operator's published times.

Q8  Can Taian Table be booked for the trip dates, and what does it cost?

Impact: HIGH VALUE — the region's only 3★

What is already verified: The official MICHELIN page states the restaurant manages its own bookings and must be contacted directly. The official site is sh.taian-table.cn and the phone is +86 173 0160 5350. The official Menu No.55 states the seasonal 10-course degustation is “fixed and mandatory” and prices add-ons at ¥388, ¥218 and ¥188.

What is not known: The degustation price is not in the official PDF, and no availability calendar was reachable.

How to discover it: Contact the restaurant directly through the official site or phone, ask for the current degustation price per guest and for availability in the chosen window, and request written confirmation. Also fetch the official wine-list PDF linked from the same site if a pairing is wanted.

Q11  What is the Chinese tourist visa fee and processing time for U.S. citizens?

Impact: BLOCKING for timing the application

What is already verified: travel.state.gov confirms a visa is generally required, that most U.S. citizens may apply for a 10-year multiple-entry tourist visa, that the passport must be valid at least 6 months beyond arrival with at least 2 blank pages, and that arrival registration within 24 hours is required.

What is not known: Fee, processing time and appointment availability are not published on that page.

How to discover it: Use the official Chinese Visa Application Service Center site for the U.S. jurisdiction and the Chinese embassy or consulate page; record the fee schedule and current processing times with the date checked.

Q17  Can foreign payment cards be used at the venues and for tickets?

Impact: MEDIUM — practical execution risk

What is already verified: Yu Garden sells tickets through its official WeChat mini program and at gate windows. The Shanghai Tower operator names five Chinese platforms as official ticket channels. The museum names trip.com for overseas visitors. The MICHELIN filter records card acceptance per restaurant (for example 132 of the Shanghai entries accept credit or debit cards, 13 are cash only, 6 do not accept foreign credit cards).

What is not known: Whether a specific U.S. card works on a specific Chinese platform was not verified.

How to discover it: Use the MICHELIN per-restaurant card flags for dining, and for tickets prefer the channels each venue officially names for overseas visitors. Confirm card acceptance directly with each venue before relying on it.
