Terminology, because it matters for your ranking.
  • MICHELIN Star — a rated distinction (1★, 2★, 3★). This is what most people mean by "Michelin restaurant". 50 in Shanghai.
  • Bib Gourmand — not a star. An award for good food at moderate prices. 34 in Shanghai. Listed separately below.
  • MICHELIN Selected — not a star. Merely "good cooking"; appears in the Guide with no distinction. 70 in Shanghai. Listed separately below.

Why the cost column is blank. You asked for a cost per couple, ideally exact. Across all ten venues above, the MICHELIN Guide publishes only a relative price band (¥ to ¥¥¥¥) — never a number. The official restaurant sites publish menus without prices, or no menu at all. Booking.com and OpenTable carry no rate for these rooms either. Filling that column would mean inventing figures, so it is left empty and marked unverified. Resolution path is on Open Questions Q4.
