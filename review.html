Verification method

Research checked 25 November 2026 against the linked official operator, government or Michelin sources. Published restaurant stars are current page observations, not promises for 2027. Menu arithmetic explicitly multiplies the operator’s per-person reference price by two. Unverified fields are displayed as such; they do not signify zero or unavailability.

External sites can change, move, redirect, require JavaScript, region access or an account. Search links are for discovery, not factual evidence. The dataset is deliberately a limited sample, not a complete national search or a verified best-value ranking.
