Each entry carries an official source link, the days it is open, its hours, any known exceptions, and a video reference. Where an official source does not state a fact, the cell says so rather than guessing.

The Monday problem. Two of the four headline attractions close on Mondays — the Shanghai Museum and Yu Garden both. A 7-night trip spanning 15–22 Feb 2027 contains one Monday (15 Feb), which is also your arrival day. Schedule accordingly; the itinerary page does exactly this.

Shanghai Museum on People's Square verified

The best-value hour of the trip if your hotel is the Radisson Blu — the museum sits on People's Square itself, a few minutes' walk away.

Important catch. The official page states that while On Top of the World Tree: Ancient Civilizations of the Americas runs (9 Jul 2026 – 14 Nov 2027), this building will host no other exhibitions. Visitors wanting the permanent collections or other special exhibitions are directed to Shanghai Museum East at 1952 Century Avenue, Pudong. Since your dates fall inside that window, you will see the Americas exhibition here and nothing else — worth knowing before you plan two museum visits.

Getting there, from the official page: Metro Line 1, 2 or 8 to People's Square, Exit 1; or Line 14 to Dashijie, Exit 4. Those three lines are precisely the ones serving the recommended hotel, which is why the Radisson scores well on this trip.

Ticketing: advance booking via the museum's official WeChat Mini Program; overseas visitors may book through trip.com per the official page. Individual visitors should bring original ID documents. Free admission is stated for visitors with disabilities; general admission pricing was not shown on the page captured — flagged for verification.

Yu Garden verified

Officially ¥30 per adult in the off-season window (1 December – 31 March) that covers your trip, so ¥60 for two. In the peak window (1 April – 30 June, 1 September – 30 November) it is ¥40 each.

Hours are 09:00–16:30 with last entry at 16:00 — an earlier finish than most visitors expect, so do not leave it until late afternoon. Closed Mondays except national statutory holidays.

Explicit unknown. The garden's own page says Spring Festival opening hours are subject to official announcement. Chinese New Year 2027 falls on 6 February with holidays through 13 February, and your trip starts 15 February. Whether normal hours have resumed is not confirmed and should be checked before relying on it. See Q9.

Two entrances: north gate at 168 Fuyou Road (groups and individuals) and south gate at 279 Yu Yuan Lao Jie (individuals only, by the Nine-Turn Bridge).

The Bund unverified

Included because it is the obvious evening walk and costs nothing — but honesty requires flagging that no official operator page was found. There is no ticketing authority for the public promenade, so no source can confirm its hours, closures or that access is unconditional.

Practical note from verified hotel descriptions: the Waldorf Astoria and Peninsula both describe themselves as waterfront/Bund properties, and Booking.com lists the Waldorf as a 5-minute drive from Nanjing Road. If you stay at the Radisson, the Bund is a short ride east.

Taian Table — the 3★ centrepiece hours unverified

Shanghai's only three-star restaurant, and the reason many people make this trip. The structure of the meal is verified: a mandatory seasonal 10-course menu (occasionally 12), served at counter seats around an open kitchen, changing every few weeks.

What is not published anywhere official: the menu price, the opening days, the seating times, or whether it closes for Chinese New Year. The official site carries the current menu as a PDF but no prices. Its booking note on the MICHELIN Guide says the restaurant manages its own bookings — so this has to be a phone call to +86 173 0160 5350.

Menu No.55 does list add-on dish prices (¥218 and ¥388), which is the only hard pricing signal available and covers two optional supplements, not the menu itself.
