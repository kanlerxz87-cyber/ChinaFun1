Notes per property

Radisson Blu Hotel Shanghai New World — the pick verified

Directly facing People's Square, and Booking.com describes it as a one-minute stroll from People's Square Subway Station, served by Lines 1, 2 and 8. That single fact does most of the work in the proximity model: three intersecting lines is what makes the west-side MICHELIN cluster reachable inside your hour.

The Superior Room is 323 ft² and lets you select one queen bed, with an attached bathroom containing both a bath and a shower. Both hard requirements pass.

One caveat worth knowing: the spa is flagged temporarily closed on the page captured. The pool, gym and sauna are listed as available.

Pullman Shanghai Jing'an — the cheapest that qualifies

At $708 for the week it undercuts the Radisson by $214 while still delivering a king bed and a private bathroom in a 334 ft² Superior King. Guest score is 8.9 from 1,097 reviews.

It is excluded from the proximity ranking because its coordinates could not be verified from an independent geocoder — geocoding returned a district centroid rather than the building. Ranking it on a guessed location would be exactly the kind of invented value this project forbids.

The Langham, Shanghai, Xintiandi — best dining base, unbookable

The Langham wins the proximity model outright: T'ang Court, its own 2★ Cantonese restaurant, is 470 m away, and all four other verified-star restaurants sit within 3.9 km. But Booking.com has no 7-night availability for 15–22 Feb 2027 — it offered only 4-, 5- and 6-night alternatives from $932.96, $1,176 and $1,419.04 respectively.

If you want it, the route is to contact the hotel directly or try a different date window. That is open question Q1.

Capella Shanghai, Jian Ye Li

Highest guest score in the set (9.6) but also no 7-night availability for the preferred window, and only 125 reviews. Not pursued further.
