Why the days are ordered this way

  • Nothing ticketed on day 1. It is a Monday, and both headline attractions close on Mondays. Arrival day is also unpredictable, so it is the correct place to absorb that.
  • Museum on day 2, garden on day 3. Both need to fall on Tue–Sun. Spreading them across two days avoids cramming two half-days together and leaves the afternoon free.
  • The two restaurants with verified hours are placed on days those hours allow. Fu He Hui and Yi Long Court are the only shortlisted venues where the MICHELIN Guide publishes opening times, and both are open daily, so Fu He Hui can anchor day 6 with certainty. The others are placeholders until you call.
  • A buffer day before departure. Day 7 is deliberately empty. If a restaurant falls through because of a Chinese New Year closure, this is where it gets rebooked.
