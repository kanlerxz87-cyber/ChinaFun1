The five decisions

These are not research tasks. They are choices only you can make, and the rest of the plan flexes around them.

Q1 · Langham quality, or Radisson value? blocking

Known. The Langham ranks #1 on cluster density (12.8 min mean modelled transit, 11 stars reachable) and has a 2★ restaurant, T'ang Court, in the building. The Radisson Blu ranks #2 (14.8 min, same 11 stars) and costs $922 for 7 nights.

Missing. The Langham has no 7-night availability on your exact dates. Alternates found: 4-night and 6-night stays at $932.96 / $1,176 / $1,419.04. So comparing it to the Radisson on a like-for-like 7-night basis is impossible.

How to close it. Decide whether a split stay (Langham 4 nights + a cheaper hotel 3 nights) is acceptable. If yes, the Langham premium becomes affordable. If no, the Radisson is the answer by default. This is the single decision that most changes the trip.

Q2 · How firm is the 15–18 February preference? blocking

Known. Chinese New Year 2027 is Saturday 6 February. The public holiday block runs 5–13 February, with Sunday 14 February designated a make-up working day. Your preferred window starts 15 February — the first working Monday after the holiday.

Implication. You are arriving the day after the holiday, which is the cheapest post-peak moment and also a quiet one. Verified Radisson ladder: 15 Feb $922 → 18 Feb $1,013 → 22 Feb $1,191 → 1 Mar $1,367 → 15 Mar $1,440. Earlier is cheaper, monotonically.

Missing. Whether travelling during the 5–13 February holiday would be cheaper still, or much worse (many restaurants close, crowds, surcharges). Not tested.

How to close it. Check the Radisson rate for 8–15 February. If it is materially lower, that is a genuine saving; weigh it against the risk of venue closures.

Q3 · Is a queen bed actually guaranteed? blocking

Known. Booking.com lists a queen bed as selectable on the Radisson Blu rate, and the $922 rate was captured on that basis. "Selectable" is not the same as "assigned".

Missing. Confirmation that the $922 rate specifically carries a queen-or-larger bed, and whether a private bathroom is ensuite at that grade.

How to close it. At checkout, select the queen option explicitly and confirm the room type appears in the confirmation email, not just the search filter.

Q4 · What will dinner actually cost? blocking

Known. Nothing useful. Not one of the twelve shortlisted restaurants publishes a menu price on its MICHELIN page or its own site. Taian Table's official site publishes the current menu as a PDF with no price. The only hard numbers anywhere are Taian Table's optional add-ons at ¥218 and ¥388.

Missing. Per-person menu price at each venue, and whether service charge applies.

How to close it. Phone each venue using the script on the itinerary page. Numbers are all in the restaurant table. This is unavoidable and cannot be done from published sources.

Q5 · Will Taian Table keep your dates? blocking

Known. Shanghai's only 3★. Mandatory 10-course menu (sometimes 12), counter seating, menu rotating every few weeks. Phone +86 173 0160 5350, manages its own bookings.

Missing. Opening days, seating times, Chinese New Year closure, deposit policy, and price. All unpublished.

How to close it. Call as soon as your dates are fixed. If it is closed 17–21 February, the itinerary day 3 slot moves and the buffer day absorbs it — which is why the buffer exists.

The six data gaps

Q6 · Where exactly are the Pullman Jing'an and Capella? data

Known. The Pullman Shanghai Jing'an is the cheapest verified hotel at $708 for 7 nights, with a king bed and an 8.9 review score. Capella Shanghai is the most expensive and had only 4-night availability.

Missing. Verified building coordinates. Geocoding the Pullman returned a district centroid — a plausible-looking point that would have produced a confident but false ranking. It was excluded rather than guessed.

How to close it. Open the hotel's location on its own site or in a maps app and read the coordinates off the pin. Then re-run python3 tools/proximity_model.py — it is designed to accept new coordinates.

This matters: at $708 the Pullman undercuts the recommendation by $214. If it turns out to be well located, it wins on price.

Q7 · Are there cheaper weeks you have not seen? data

Known. Five windows were sampled for the Radisson: 15 Feb, 18 Feb, 22 Feb, 1 Mar, 15 Mar — all increasing.

Missing. Everything outside those five. The trend is monotonic across the sampled points, but the sample is five points, not a season.

How to close it. Price the Radisson across the full 15 Feb – 23 Mar range in a single Booking.com search using the calendar view. Any dip below $922 changes the recommendation.

Q8 · Is $922 really dollars? data

Known. All totals are as displayed by Booking.com with the US/English locale, i.e. USD, taxes and charges included per the listing.

Missing. The exchange rate on the day you book, and whether your card adds a foreign-transaction fee.

How to close it. Nothing to research — just budget a small margin. Figures are comparative, so the ranking holds regardless.

Q9 · Is Yu Garden on normal hours during your dates? data

Known. 09:00–16:30, last entry 16:00, closed Mondays except national statutory holidays. ¥30 per adult off-season (1 Dec – 31 Mar).

Missing. The garden states that Spring Festival hours are set by official announcement. Whether normal hours have resumed by 17 February is not confirmed.

How to close it. Check yugarden.com.cn shortly before departure, or phone the garden.

Q10 · What does the Shanghai Museum cost, and which building? data

Known. Tue–Sun 09:00–17:00, last entry 15:00, closed Mondays. Metro Line 1/2/8 People's Square Exit 1, or Line 14 Dashijie Exit 4. During the Americas exhibition (to 14 Nov 2027) the People's Square building shows nothing else.

Missing. General admission price — the visit page states free entry only for visitors with disabilities. Evening-session availability (09:00–21:00) requires the museum's WeChat.

How to close it. Book through the official WeChat Mini Program, which shows the price at booking. Overseas visitors are told to use trip.com.

Q11 · Does the Bund have hours at all? data

Known. It is a public promenade. No official operator page exists.

Missing. Anything authoritative about access, closures or crowd control — including whether there are Spring Festival restrictions.

How to close it. Treat as unrestricted, but confirm locally at the hotel on arrival. This is a genuinely low-risk unknown.
