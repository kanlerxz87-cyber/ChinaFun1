Source hierarchy applied. Operator's own site > MICHELIN Guide > official municipal/tourism body > booking platform. A booking platform was accepted for prices and review scores (it is the seller), never for museum hours. Where the top two disagreed, both are shown — see F-01.

Reproducing this

The proximity model is plain Python with no dependencies beyond the standard library:

python3 shanghai-2027/tools/proximity_model.py

It reads data/hotels.json and data/michelin-restaurants.json, applies the constants declared at the top of the file, and writes data/hotel-ranking.json. Change the constants and the ranking recomputes — the assumptions are visible and adjustable rather than buried in a spreadsheet.

Data files: hotels.json · michelin-restaurants.json · activities.json · price-windows.json · hotel-ranking.json
