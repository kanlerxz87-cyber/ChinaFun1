Why no hotel-to-venue minutes appear on this site The brief asks for activities within one hour of the hotel by subway or bus. That is a routing claim, and no official Shanghai Metro journey planner or routing API was reachable in this session. Rather than publish a plausible-looking number, the site uses the two signals that are fully verifiable — shared district from official addresses, and the operator's own published journey times above — and flags the rest. See Open Questions Q7 and Suggestion S4 for how to close the gap.
