What this means for booking

  1. Do not book anything non-refundable before the visa is in hand. The advisory is Level 2 and the visa is a pre-travel requirement; the Waldorf's cheapest rate is explicitly non-refundable and is charged in full at booking.
  2. Monday 15 Feb 2027 is a low-value sightseeing day. Three headline venues are closed. Use it for arrival, the Bund promenade and hotel check-in.
  3. Saturday 20 Feb 2027 is the highest-value and highest-crowd day (Lantern Festival). Book timed entries in advance where the venue offers them.
  4. The cheapest verified stay window is 8–15 Mar 2027, which loses the Lantern Festival and gains $40.
