Executive Summary

Shanghai is the only city in the requested window that satisfies the MICHELIN-dining requirement at the top end: the 2026 guide's single three-star restaurant for the whole Shanghai–Jiangsu–Zhejiang region is here, and the official guide lists 153 Shanghai establishments.

Prepared 2026-11-25. 2 adults, 1 room, 7 nights. All prices are live dated quotes; all hours are quoted from official pages.

1 · Recommendation in one paragraph

Base in Huangpu district for 7 nights, arriving Monday 15 February 2027. Huangpu holds the densest officially-addressed MICHELIN cluster verified in this session — five guide entries including the two-star T'ang Court and the one-star Lu Style — and both Bund hotels sit inside it, so the ≤1-hour activity rule is met without relying on unverifiable transit times. Book the Waldorf Astoria Shanghai on the Bund (2 queen beds, marble bathroom with separate tub and shower — the requirement is met verbatim) at $2,374 for 7 nights in the 8–15 March window, or hold the preferred February week at $2,414. If budget leads, the Radisson Collection Hyland king room is $696 for 7 nights while the live 30% promotion runs — but its bathroom type is not stated on the rate page and must be confirmed before booking. Spend Monday 15 February on arrival and the Bund promenade, because Yu Garden, the People's Square museum and T'ang Court are all closed that day.

7 · Risks and constraints

Entry and legal U.S. State Department advisory for mainland China is Level 2 – Exercise increased caution, issued 19 Oct 2026 (wrongful detention, exit bans, arbitrary enforcement of local laws). A visa is generally required; most U.S. citizens may apply for a 10-year multiple-entry tourist visa. Passport must be valid at least 6 months beyond arrival with at least 2 blank pages. You must register within 24 hours of arrival and re-register at every new location.
