Two distinct answers to “cheapest time” Cheapest stay window: Mon 8 – Mon 15 Mar 2027 ($2,374 at the Waldorf vs $2,414 for the preferred week, −$40 / −1.7%). Cheapest time to book: now. The Radisson rate of $696 is a 30% “Black Friday Deal” observed live on 2026-11-25 whose published validity is “select stays between Nov 19, 2026 and Dec 31, 2027” — a range that covers every date in the trip window. The same room without the promotion is $994.49, so booking during the promotion saves $298.35, which is roughly seven times the entire saving available from date-shifting.
The real cost driver is cancellation flexibility, not dates At the Waldorf in the preferred week, moving from non-refundable ($2,414) to free cancellation ($3,018) costs +$604 — 15× the $40 available from choosing the cheapest week. Decide the flexibility question first.
