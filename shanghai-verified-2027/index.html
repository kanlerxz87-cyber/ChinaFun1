One scheduling conflict dominates the plan The preferred start date Mon 15 Feb 2027 is a Monday. Yu Garden, the Shanghai Museum on People's Square and the two-star T'ang Court are all officially closed / off on Mondays. Treat that day as arrival + Bund promenade, or shift the 4-day block to Thu 18 – Sun 21 Feb, which also captures the Lantern Festival on Sat 20 Feb 2027.
