How to book, officially

  • Taian Table (3★) — the official MICHELIN page states “This establishment manages its own bookings. Please contact the restaurant directly to book a table.” Official site sh.taian-table.cn, phone +86 173 0160 5350.
  • Ren He Guan (1★) — same “manages its own bookings” notice; phone +86 21 6403 0731.
  • T'ang Court (2★) — reserve via DiningCity or (8621) 2330 2430. Official notes: tell them any dietary or seating requirements when booking, give a contact number (international guests should supply a local or hotel number), be punctual — reserved tables are held only 15 minutes. Seating: 24 à la carte guests, 91 in private dining rooms. Smart casual attire.
  • Guide-wide booking partners — MICHELIN's own footer lists OpenTable and Resy partnerships: OpenTable · Resy.
