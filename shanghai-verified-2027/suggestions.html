T4Enter the People's Square museum before 15:00, not 16:00The current official visit page states last entry at 15:00; an older official notice said 16:00. The newer page governs — irregularity R6.
T5Buy Yu Garden tickets as off-seasonOfficial schedule: ¥30 per adult from 1 Dec to 31 Mar, which covers every date in the trip window; ¥40 only applies 1 Apr – 30 Jun and 1 Sep – 30 Nov.

4 · How to record a decision

  1. Write the chosen path letter into the Decision column of the path table, and the chosen suggestions into their Decision columns (accept / reject / defer).
  2. Log the same decision in COORDINATION.md under a new Decisions heading, with the date and the session that made it.
  3. Close the blocking questions the chosen path depends on, using the discovery routes on Open Questions, and add each new value with its source URL and fetch date to the matching file in /data.
  4. Only then implement: re-run the dated hotel quote for the chosen path, confirm the live total, and book.
