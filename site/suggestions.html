Suggestions to improve decision-making

  • Heat map of Michelin density vs budget per hotel. Add a ranking page. Score = starred-restaurant count within ~1h (approx.) + per-star multipliers — first review the approximations on Hotels.
  • Booking-timeline check. For each of the top-3 hotels per city, fetch Booking.com pages with 3 different dates (Feb 15, Mar 1, Mar 8) and compare totals — best "cheapest time" evidence.
  • Day-by-day itinerary. Draft a 14-day timeline (3 nights Livefortuna/Hotel Éclat + 11 nights Shanghai) with activities, restaurants, transport from hotels. First collect preferences on Questions.
  • Verify queen-bed rooms. For Livefortuna (official says "1 queen-size bed") and Andaz Xintiandi (add "queen room" to itinerary; currently flagged), review with hotel owners/Booking.com filters before deciding.
  • Budget checks for restaurants. Fetch restaurant booking pages (guide.michelin.com) or Tripadvis/Booking.com for the 8 starred restaurants to confirm price ranges — many marked "flag".
  • Create a comparison table. Ranking: Livefortuna (Beijing, ~3 starred), Hotel Éclat Beijing (Dongcheng), NUO (none flagged), The Langham Xintiandi (Shanghai, 2 starred), Andaz Xintiandi, InterContinental Ruijin — compare on: Michelin density, price, quirkiness, location vs activities.
  • Timeline question list. Before implementing, review with the couple (see Questions & Info Gaps).
