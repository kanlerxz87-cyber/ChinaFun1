Executive summary

  1. The repo already contained four complete, independently verified trip plans before this session: one Beijing plan at the repo root and three Shanghai plans in separate folders. This study adds the piece the brief emphasised most but the plans covered least: a source-checked register of social-video reviews alongside every listed venue.
  2. 58 social-video links were verified for 25 of the 43 venues in the Beijing plan: 31 links across 15 of the 33 MICHELIN-starred restaurants, and 27 links across all 10 activities. Each link was confirmed by YouTube's own oEmbed endpoint returning the exact video title and channel; no link was accepted from a search snippet alone.
  3. Every one of the 10 activities has at least one verified video. Coverage for restaurants is uneven: three-star and two-star venues have 3 videos each, while 18 restaurants have none that can be tied to the exact venue without guessing.
  4. No TikTok or Instagram link could be verified from a first-party page in this pass. They are left blank and flagged rather than filled with unverified handles or search URLs.
  5. Opening hours could be confirmed for only four of 43 venues (King's Joy, Trb Hutong, Palace Museum and Mutianyu). All other hour cells are blank and flagged. Video descriptions are not treated as official opening-hour evidence.
  6. This study does not re-decide the city, hotel or dates. Those choices remain open across the four existing plans, and the questions page states exactly what must be answered first.

How a link became “verified”

  1. Find candidate videos on YouTube search (example query) ↗ — one query per venue, logged in research-log.md.
  2. Fetch YouTube oEmbed (example) ↗ for the exact video ID; it returns the canonical title and channel.
  3. Store the link only if the returned title names the venue. Otherwise leave the cell blank and flag it.

oEmbed confirms the video exists and its published title/channel. It does not confirm upload date, the reviewer's qualifications, or that the content is flattering. Star counts spoken in older videos are not used as authority for the 2027 guide status. Search-query URLs are not counted as review links. No TikTok or Instagram account was asserted because neither platform could be verified from a first-party page in this pass.
