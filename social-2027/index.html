What the evidence says

  • Social coverage is the gap. Only 29 of 83 venues (35%) have a YouTube, TikTok or Instagram link that was actually fetched and matched to the venue. 54 venues have none and are listed as blank.
  • Six inherited links were search pages, not reviews. They are rejected and replaced where a direct video could be verified; 13 candidate links in total were rejected: search-result pages, wrong-city/wrong-business accounts and false matches.
  • Hours are mostly unpublished. 12 of 83 venues (14%) have hours confirmed from an official page this session. The rest are blank — including famous restaurants whose MICHELIN entry shows no hours at all.
  • Exact dinner prices are rare. Only 4 venues publish a price for two. MICHELIN's ¥ band is a spending category, not a price: it is never presented as a total here.
  • Creator quotes are not prices. Reviewers mention ¥988, ¥2,888 and ¥7,500 figures; all are labelled unofficial and excluded from the cost column.
