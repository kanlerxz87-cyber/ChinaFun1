Which city should the trip be in? The brief does not say.
Four plans exist: one Beijing, three Shanghai. They optimise different things and disagree with each other.
 Not decidable from the evidence. Ask the traveller to rank three priorities: star count, flight cost, or hotel value; then select the owning plan. No new research needed.
Do the dates work? 15–18 Feb 2027 is the preferred window; the brief also asks for 1–2 weeks.
Three nights vs one to two weeks. All plans use 15–22 Feb 2027 (seven nights).
 No — the preferred window is too short. Decide whether 15 Feb is a fixed arrival date or merely a preferred start; if fixed, Monday closures apply on day one. See q3.
Is 15 Feb 2027 a Monday, and does it matter? Yes — computed with the standard calendar.
Palace Museum, Yu Garden, Shanghai Museum (People's Square) and T'ang Court publish Monday closure; Da Vittorio shows Monday closed.
 Yes: the first day cannot be a museum/garden day. Rebuild the day-one plan around an open venue, or arrive a day earlier.
Which hotel is genuinely cheapest for the exact week? Not answered by this session.
Other plans quote $696, $922 and $1,116 seven-night totals for different room types.
 Unresolved — do not treat any one figure as final. Re-quote the same room type on each hotel's own booking engine for 15–22 Feb 2027, with taxes shown.
Does the cheapest room meet 'queen bed or larger, private bathroom'? Partially.
One plan explicitly flags its cheapest $652 room as twin beds and excludes it. Others name a queen room with attached bath.
 Needs a room-type check at booking. Capture a screenshot of the room page showing bed and bathroom before comparing prices.
Are the star statuses current? Partly.

 No for Shanghai 2027. Recheck the MICHELIN guide after the 2027 Shanghai release and update status fields.
How many starred restaurants are within one hour of each hotel by subway or bus? Not answered.
One plan publishes a modelled estimate with disclosed constants; another refuses to model and leaves the field blank. No routed journey time was fetched in this session.
 Unanswered. Run the same origin–destination set through Amap or Baidu Maps at the hour of travel and store the route URL with each result.

T'ang Court ¥888 / ¥2,288 for two; Yu Garden and Palace Museum totals are calculated from published tariffs; TRB Hutong calculated from its menu. MICHELIN ¥ bands are not prices.
 Mostly unknown. Request current menus by email or check each restaurant's own menu PDF; record the date of the quote.
Which venues publish opening days, hours and exceptions? 12 of 83 confirmed here.
The rest have no fetched official hours statement. Where a plan carried hours but this session could not refetch them, the value is blank and flagged.
 Insufficient for scheduling. Re-check each restaurant's own site and MICHELIN page in the week before travel; record a source URL per cell.

 Unverified. Locate the State Council holiday notice on gov.cn and link it before treating any date as a holiday.
